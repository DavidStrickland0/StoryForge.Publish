# Canon

## Established Canon


- Adrian Vale is nineteen years old.


- Adrian Vale's parents are deceased.


- Adrian Vale has no family support.


- Adrian Vale is a freshman in a general studies program.


- Adrian Vale is enrolled in a university with a financial aid office and a community resource center.


- The university's emergency fund is allocated for immediate medical crises, housing emergencies resulting from natural disasters, or documented family tragedies.


- Adrian Vale's emergency hardship application was denied because his situation was categorized as insufficient initial funding.


- Adrian Vale has a lease agreement and a tuition invoice.


- Adrian Vale has a deadline to cover his financial balance by Friday to maintain student status and housing.


- Marcus is an acquaintance of Adrian Vale who works at The Gilded Cage.


- Apex Research Institute operates an Experimental Medicine Program.


- The Experimental Medicine Program offers competitive compensation, free medical care, and flexible scheduling in exchange for participation in low-risk studies.


- Apex Research Institute has active trials for sleep disorders, mild hypertension, and male fertility.


- The male-fertility trial involves taking a daily medication and providing a sample daily.


- The male-fertility trial offers the highest compensation among the institute's programs.


- Participants in the male-fertility trial can withdraw at any time without questions.
