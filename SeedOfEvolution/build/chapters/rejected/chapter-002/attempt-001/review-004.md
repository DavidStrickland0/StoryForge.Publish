## Revision Guidance

- Remove the depiction of Adrian receiving his first dose of medication and providing his first baseline sample, as these events have already occurred in Chapter 1.
- Advance the narrative to the second day or later, depicting Adrian's experience with the ongoing daily routine, side effects, and academic pressures, rather than repeating the intake process.
- Ensure the clinic's staff and layout are consistent with the established canon, using the same receptionist and intake office as in Chapter 1.
- Avoid introducing new, unsupported details about Adrian's academic schedule, phone, or family support, as these contradict his established isolation and financial struggles.
- Focus on the psychological and physical toll of the ongoing trial, the conflict between his academic responsibilities and the trial's demands, and the escalating side effects, rather than repeating the initial intake and first-dose events.
- Revise the medication administration scene so that Dr. Thorne does not consume the medication herself. Instead, she should instruct Adrian to take the dose, or demonstrate the *method* (e.g., showing how to open the vial) without ingesting the contents, ensuring Adrian receives the full dose as required by the trial protocol.
- Clarify the spatial and temporal logic of the 'one-hour wait.' If Adrian is required to stay in a specific area for monitoring, describe him remaining in that area (e.g., a waiting room or observation chair) for the full hour before being called for the sample. If the sample is part of the immediate post-administration protocol, adjust the dialogue to reflect that the sample is collected *during* the observation period, not after a separate hour of waiting in a different location.
