# Validated Revision Findings

## Blocking

Category: PhysicalLogic

Adrian's phone is described as being left on the desk in Room 4 to charge, but he is seen checking his phone at 10:15 AM in the bathroom, and then later in the hallway he checks his phone again ('Adrian checked his phone. The battery icon was a small red sliver. He had left it on his desk in Room 4, charging.'). This is a direct contradiction. He cannot check his phone if he left it on the desk. The text says he 'checked his phone' in the bathroom at 10:15 AM. Then, after leaving the clinic, he 'checked his phone' again and realized he left it on the desk. This is a continuity error in the physical state of the object. He cannot have the phone in his hand to check the time, and then later claim he left it on the desk.

Validation: The chapter explicitly states Adrian checked his phone in the bathroom at 10:15 AM, implying he was holding it. Later, after leaving the clinic, the text states he 'checked his phone' and realized he had left it on the desk in Room 4. One cannot check a phone that is not in one's possession. This is a direct physical contradiction regarding the location of the object.
