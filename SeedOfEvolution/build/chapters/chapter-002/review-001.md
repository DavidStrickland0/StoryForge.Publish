# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains significant continuity errors regarding the timeline and events of the fertility trial, contradicting the established canon from Chapter 1. Specifically, it depicts Adrian receiving a second dose of medication and performing a second sample collection on the same day he already completed his first dose and baseline sample. Additionally, the chapter introduces a new conflict (schedule conflict with classes) that was not established in the previous chapter, creating a logical inconsistency in Adrian's knowledge and planning. The narrative also fails to advance the active Short arc's NextMilestone, which requires Adrian to navigate the second week of the trial and deal with side effects, but instead repeats the intake process.
