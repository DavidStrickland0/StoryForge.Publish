# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains significant continuity errors regarding the timeline and events of the fertility trial, specifically contradicting the established canon that Adrian completed his first day's protocol (medication and sample) in the previous chapter. It also introduces a logical contradiction regarding Adrian's academic schedule and the trial's requirements, and fails to advance the active narrative arc by repeating the intake process rather than progressing to the next milestone (signing the emergency authorization or experiencing side effects).

## Findings

- [Continuity] The chapter depicts Adrian receiving his first dose of medication and providing a baseline sample, which directly contradicts the established canon and the previous chapter where these events were already completed.
  Evidence: In the previous chapter, Adrian states: 'The intake payment has been deposited to your account. Your next appointment is tomorrow, 8:00 AM.' and 'Adrian has received the first dose of the trial medication.' In the current chapter, Dr. Thorne says: 'Then you are cleared for today’s dose.' and Adrian takes a vial: 'Adrian took the vial. He uncapped it. He tilted his head back and let the liquid slide down his throat.' This repeats the intake event as if it is happening for the first time, ignoring that he already completed Day 1.
- [Continuity] The chapter implies Adrian is still in the intake/first-day phase, whereas the canon establishes he has already completed his first day and is scheduled for his second day.
  Evidence: The chapter states: 'He had about forty-five minutes before he had to be back in Room 4 for the final sign-off and the administrative processing of his daily attendance.' This suggests he is still in the initial intake process. However, the canon states: 'Adrian Vale has completed his first day of the trial protocol.' and 'Adrian Vale has a scheduled appointment for the next day at 8:00 AM.' The chapter treats the current visit as the first one, not the second.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter fails to advance the active Short arc by repeating the intake process instead of progressing to the next milestone, which is signing the emergency-intervention authorization or experiencing the side effects of the medication.
  Evidence: The active Short arc 'epic-001-081-long-1-medium-1-short-1' has a NextMilestone: 'Adrian signs the emergency-intervention authorization and enrolls in the experimental-medicine program to secure the financial payments necessary to keep his college enrollment active.'
