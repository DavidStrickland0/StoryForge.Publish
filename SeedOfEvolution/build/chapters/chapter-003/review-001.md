# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter successfully completes the enrollment milestone by having Adrian sign the agreement, receive payment, and perform the first daily routine. However, it contains a significant continuity error regarding the trial's capacity and a repetitive narrative structure that undermines the emotional weight of the scene. The prose also suffers from redundant internal monologue and a mechanical pacing in the climax that feels rushed despite the tension.

## Revision Guidance

- Clarify the participant ID numbering to align with the '14 active participants' canon. If Adrian is the 15th, use V-015. If he is replacing the 14th, clarify the ID system.
- Remove the repeated internal monologue paragraphs to avoid redundancy and maintain narrative momentum.
- Revise the payment description to align with the canon of 'paid on the first of the month.' If the first payment is immediate, clarify that it is a prorated amount or a different type of payment.
- Enhance the emotional weight of the collection scene by focusing on Adrian's internal experience and the 'degrading' nature of the trial, rather than a detached, clinical description.
- Vary the narrative structure to avoid circular repetition and show the progression of Adrian's emotional state after the first routine.
