# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter successfully completes the enrollment milestone but suffers from severe structural repetition and continuity errors. The narrative loops back to the pharmacy and collection room after already depicting the first dose and sample, creating a disjointed timeline. Additionally, the prose contains significant typos and redundant internal monologue that undermines the character's established voice.
