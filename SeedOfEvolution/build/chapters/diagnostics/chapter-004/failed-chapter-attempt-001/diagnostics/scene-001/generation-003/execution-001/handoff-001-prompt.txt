"Room 4," she said. Her voice was flat, devoid of the usual customer-service lilt. "Dr. Thorne is expecting you."

Adrian nodded, keeping his face blank. He knew the route. He had walked it yesterday, and the day before, but the clinic felt different today. He kept his head down, focusing on the tip of his shoes as he moved past the magazine rack. The pages were glossy and bright, offering articles about wellness and diet, a stark, mocking contrast to the gnawing hollow in his stomach.

Now, the hunger had transformed into a low-grade nausea, a churning sensation in his gut that threatened to surface with every step. He gripped the strap of his bag, his knuckles white, fighting the tremor that was starting to shake his hand. His fingers were useless.
