Adrian swallowed, the motion feeling thick and difficult in his throat. His stomach gave a low, hollow rumble, a sound that seemed absurdly loud in the quiet room. It spoke of "unrestricted access," "biological samples," and "emergency intervention" in a tone that suggested his body was no more than a container for their research, a vessel that could be opened, examined, and discarded if it failed to perform.

Dr. Thorne’s gaze remained fixed on him. She did not look away, nor did she look concerned. Her expression was neutral, clinical, a mask of professional detachment that Adrian had come to recognize as a shield. She was not a doctor who worried about her patients; she was a researcher who monitored her subjects. And right now, he was failing to perform.
