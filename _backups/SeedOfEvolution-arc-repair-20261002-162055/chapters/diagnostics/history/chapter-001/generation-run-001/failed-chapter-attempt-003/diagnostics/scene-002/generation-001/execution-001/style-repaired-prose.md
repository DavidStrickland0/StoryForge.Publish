The punch came fast.

Adrian saw it—somehow saw it—a blur of muscle and rage hurtling toward his face. His body moved before his mind registered the threat. He twisted, feeling the air displacement as Marcus's fist whipped past his ear close enough to leave a sting.

*How did I—*

No time. Marcus was already pivoting, committed to the follow-through, his momentum carrying him forward. Adrian's instincts screamed a warning he couldn't articulate. He dropped low, his bad shoulder howling in protest, and drove his head into Marcus's midsection.

The impact was ugly. The collision was brutally messy. Adrian's forehead connected with Marcus's ribs. Something cracked—not bone, maybe cartilage. Marcus grunted, the breath forced from his lungs in a sharp exhale. He stumbled backward, crashing into the pole beside the driver's seat.

The bus tilted as it rounded a corner, and Marcus slipped, his feet scrabbling for purchase on the uneven floor. He caught himself against the pole, but the moment of disorientation was enough.

Adrian didn't think. He surged forward, his swollen knuckles connecting with Marcus's jaw. The blow wasn't clean—his stance was wrong, his shoulder screaming with each movement—but it landed. Marcus's head snapped sideways. His eyes rolled back.

He went down hard.

The bus screeched to a halt, brakes shrieking. Passengers lurched in their seats. The elderly woman gasped. The young couple grabbed each other for balance.

Marcus lay motionless on the floor, blood trickling from his split lip.

Adrian stood over him, chest heaving, vision swimming. His shoulder felt like it had been wrenched from its socket. His knuckles were purple and swelling. His lip was cut where Marcus had caught him earlier.

But Marcus wasn't moving.

"Fuck," Adrian breathed. "Fuck, fuck, fuck."

Lena was still behind him, pressed against the front wall of the bus. Her breathing was rapid and shallow. She had one hand over her mouth, her eyes wide.

The bus driver was on the radio, his voice tight. "Officer, I have a situation here. Two men fighting on the bus. One is unconscious. The other is... Jesus, kid, you're bleeding."

Adrian looked down at himself. Blood trickled from a cut on his cheek. He didn't remember when it had happened. His shirt was torn at the shoulder. His hands were shaking.

*I just... I just fought someone. I just put him on the floor.*

The realization hit him like a physical blow. He'd intervened. He'd fought. He'd won.

Or had he? Marcus was down, but he wasn't dead. And the police were coming.

Adrian's legs gave out. He grabbed the pole beside him, using it to steady himself. His vision swam. He could taste blood in his mouth. His heart was hammering so hard he could hear it in his ears.

"Sit down, kid," the driver said. "Sit down before you fall down."

Adrian slumped into the nearest seat, his shoulder screaming in protest. He looked at his hands. They were shaking. They'd just beaten someone. They'd just protected Lena.

*What the hell is happening to me?*
