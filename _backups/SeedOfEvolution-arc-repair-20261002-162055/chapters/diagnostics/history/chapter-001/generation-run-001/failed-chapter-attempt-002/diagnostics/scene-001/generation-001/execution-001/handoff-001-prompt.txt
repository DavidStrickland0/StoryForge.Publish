Adrian watched from across the street as Lena stepped into the pool of light cast by the corner streetlamp. A man—broad-shouldered, with an air of mean-spirited aggression—emerged from the shadows of the alley beside the convenience store. For a fleeting moment, a spark of adrenaline ignited within Adrian, a fire that singed the edges of his fear. This was his chance to do something—anything—to make the world a better, safer place. Adrian's heart slammed against his ribs, and the world seemed to slow as his mind raced to catch up with his body's desperate instincts.

Adrian's own fear gave him a wild, unreasoning courage as he stepped forward, the words tumbling out of his mouth. "You can't just hit her! She's not—she's not your property!"
