## Engagement Findings

- The chapter is slow-paced and repetitive, with excessive internal monologue that explains the protagonist's motives and feelings rather than showing them through action.
- The climax feels rushed compared to the lengthy buildup, reducing the impact of the protagonist's sacrifice.

## Revision Guidance

- Clarify the status of the infant to ensure the protagonist's act qualifies as saving a life, which is required for System initialization.
- Reduce the internal monologue and show the protagonist's character through action and dialogue rather than explanation.
- Add specific worldbuilding details to ground the scene in a realistic Earth battlefield.
- Pace the chapter to build tension more effectively, avoiding repetitive deliberation and ensuring the climax feels earned and impactful.
