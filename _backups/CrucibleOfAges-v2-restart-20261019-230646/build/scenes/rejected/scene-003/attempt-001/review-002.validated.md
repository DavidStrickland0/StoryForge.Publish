## Blocking

Category: Canon

The chapter states Caelen spent eighteen years training, implying he is 18 years old. Canon establishes he is an 18-year-old Marine, meaning he has only been in the Marines for a short time (likely 1-2 years), not 18. This misrepresents his military experience and age.

Validation: The finding correctly identifies a contradiction between the chapter's text and the established canon. The chapter claims Caelen spent 18 years training, which is impossible for an 18-year-old character. This is a material defect that misrepresents the character's background and violates the canonical age constraint.
