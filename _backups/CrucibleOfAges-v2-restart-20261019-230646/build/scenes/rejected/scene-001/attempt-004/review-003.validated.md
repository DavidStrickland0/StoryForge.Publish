Category: Style

The chapter uses a sustained pattern of clipped narrative sentences and fragments for dramatic emphasis, which is strongly discouraged by the writing rules.

Validation: The writing rules explicitly prohibit the use of one-word narrative sentences or fragments for dramatic emphasis and strongly discourage sentences of four words or fewer. The chapter repeatedly uses this pattern (e.g., 'I moved.', 'I lunged.', 'I was alive.') to create tension and emphasis, which is a sustained violation of the style guidelines.
