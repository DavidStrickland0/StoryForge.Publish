# Canon

## Established Canon


- A super with strength and invulnerability is present at The Gilded Cage bar.


- The city has laws that do not effectively cover supers, allowing them to act without consequence.


- Elena has undergone plastic surgery to maintain a youthful appearance.


- The super's name is FireMan.


- FireMan possesses slow-twitch strength and indestructible skin.


- FireMan owns a penthouse.


- FireMan's skills were neutralized when he touched David.


- Ethan Vale possesses a device called a Lens that provides augmented reality navigation.


- The government officially categorizes superpowers as 'skills'.


- Skills are permanent and cannot be turned on or off by the user.


- Skills lock onto specific organs or organ systems rather than the whole body.


- Indestructible gallstones are a known variant of the indestructibility skill.


- The date of the incident is November 4th, 2074.


- Mara exists and is an established character.


- Ben exists and is an established character.


- Mara and Ethan Vale were in a romantic relationship.


- Mara and Ethan Vale have ended their romantic relationship.


- Mara and Ethan Vale still exchange birthday messages.


- Ben and Ethan Vale have known each other since Ethan's first job.


- Ben took a job outside the city.


- Ben's contact with Ethan Vale has ceased.


- Ethan Vale lives in an apartment with a convertible sofa bed.


- Ethan Vale possesses a blue mug that belonged to Mara.


- Ethan Vale possesses a screwdriver.


- Ethan Vale's kitchen drawer runner is damaged and requires frequent tightening.


- Ethan Vale uses a Lens device that can be ejected onto a charging dock.


- Ethan Vale is a civilian who has not identified any superpowers in himself.


- The public belief is that approximately one in three people has some kind of skill.


- Skills are described as permanent and attached to specific organs or systems.


- Elastic body hair is a known variant of a skill.


- Aurora exists and is an established character.


- Aurora possesses the ability to fly.


- Ray exists and is an established character.


- Ray is a doorman at Ethan Vale's apartment building.


- Ray holds a Security Level 4 badge.


- Ethan Vale has known Ray for several years.


- Ethan Vale has admired Aurora since he was a teenager.

## Changes Over Time


- Ben's location changed from being local to Ethan to being outside the city.


- Ben's communication frequency with Ethan decreased until it stopped entirely.


- Ethan's kitchen drawer runner, which was repaired by Ben, has degraded to the point where it catches and requires manual adjustment.
