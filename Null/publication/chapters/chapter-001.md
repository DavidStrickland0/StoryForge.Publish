# Chapter 1: The Weight of the Air

The neon sign for *The Gilded Cage* buzzed with a low, irritating hum that seemed to vibrate in Ethan’s molars. He sat in the booth farthest from the main floor, his back against the vinyl, nursing a beer that had gone warm twenty minutes ago. He wasn’t drinking; he was observing. It was a habit born of necessity, or at least of the particular brand of caution that came with living in a city where the strongest people in the room were not the ones who wrote the laws.

Across the bar, near the private dining section, a woman commanded a table as if she owned the building, the staff, and the air molecules trapped between the chairs. Elena was gorgeous, a sculpted woman in her forties with a fitted suit that cost more than Ethan’s monthly rent. Her high cheeks, smooth forehead, and carefully full lips had the polished symmetry of expensive plastic surgery; the whole effect was aimed at twenty-something, though the confidence in her eyes belonged to someone who had spent decades getting her way. Her voice carried easily over the ambient noise of the bar, a sharp, clipped cadence that left no room for disagreement.

“…and I don’t care if the board says otherwise,” she said, gesturing with a glass of red wine that didn’t spill a drop. Her companion, a man with a smile that looked like it had been purchased and fitted to his face, nodded rapidly. “The merger holds. You know that, David. You know that because I made you know it.”

David turned the page of notes he had been studying face down and leaned toward her. “Of course, Elena. Your vision is… it’s the only one that matters. I just thought the legal team might have concerns, but that’s why you’re here. To handle the concerns.” When she looked his way, he lifted his glass in a small toast; she left it hanging.

Elena smirked, a thin, predatory expression that suggested she found his existence both useful and mildly amusing. “The legal team can handle the paperwork. I handle the reality. There’s a difference, David. A significant one. You should try to understand it before the next quarterly review.”

Ethan took a slow sip of his beer, his gaze dropping to the ice cubes swirling in his glass. David set his glass down untouched and nodded at Elena’s next remark before she finished it. She raised one finger when he drew breath, and he closed his mouth. Neither of them, though, had the kind of power Ethan had seen on the evening news.

The conversation between Elena and David went on her nasal voice drowning on and on about all the things the company was doing wrong at what she would have done instead. AS the night dragged on, they drank and the more Elena drank the louder she got.

Ethan’s eyes drifted past them, toward the table next to the entrance where a figure sat with the casual, arrogant posture of someone who knew that no law applied to them. He was broad-shouldered and dressed in a suit that fit his muscular frame with a tension that suggested the fabric was holding on by a thread. He wasn’t drinking. He wasn’t talking. He was just sitting there, watching Elena obviously annoyed for some reason.

Elena’s next sentence carried across the bar. “If the board wants to waste another quarter arguing, they can do it without me.” She struck the table with the flat of her hand. Silverware jumped, and a server stopped short beside the neighboring booth. The couple there turned in their seats. Elena raised her voice over the music and named a director who, she said, had never made a decision without someone else taking the blame. David reached toward her sleeve, then withdrew his hand when she glanced at it.

At the entrance table, the man laid his phone face down and rested both hands on the tabletop. Elena kept her attention on David, her wineglass lifted as she spoke; she never looked toward the entrance.

Ethan felt a cold prickle of unease crawl up his neck. The supers were celebrities, strategic assets, gods in tailored jackets. And this one was looking at Elena with the disturbed irritation of a cat watching a mouse that had made a noise.

Ethan set his beer down. His hand was steady, but his heart was beating a little faster than it should have. Every instinct he had, every rational calculation he had made in his quiet, ordinary life, screamed at him to stay out of this man’s way.

He couldn’t get involved heck he didn’t want to be anywhere near that table. He had no powers, no connections, no leverage. If he walked over there, he would be a civilian close enough to a super to be squashed. The super could break him, and no one would stop him. The police would be too slow, the lawyers too late and they were that way intentionally. The law didn’t cover supers they just covered what came after the super was done. That was the reality of the city. The strong did what they wanted, and the rest of them tried to survive.

Elena was still talking, her voice rising in pitch, her words becoming a blur of corporate jargon and threats. David smiled when she glanced at him and checked the expression the moment, she looked away. And the super was still seated by the entrance, his eyes fixed on Elena’s face, the corner of his mouth twitching in a way that promised trouble.

Ethan looked at the exit. It was past the bar and past the table where the super sat. To leave, he would have to walk right through the zone of danger. If he stayed, he would have to watch what happened next and hope the super didn’t decide to just clean house.

He took another sip of his beer, the taste metallic and stale. He waited.

The super pushed back his chair and stood, and buttoning his suit jacket as he began to move. He didn’t walk so much as glide across the carpet, his eyes locked on Elena with the detached, predatory focus of a man choosing which piece of furniture to break next. David looked shocked staring at the super. He wasn't certain what was happening but he looked scared. Elena finally caught the look in David's eye and turned to see what had him so scared.

Ethan wasn't certain if she was just too stupid to recognize the super or too stupid to care. But Elena proved the stupid part when she didn’t rise. She didn’t reach for her purse or check the exit. She just sat there, glaring at him until she finally decided on saying "What the fuck do you want." Ethan watched all the blood drain from David's face. 

"You’ve verbal assault has gone none stop for hours." the super said in a deep voice that everyone in the bar could hear. "So, what do I want? I want you to shut your damn ..." 

He was more than likely going to say 'mouth' he never got to it. As he was talking, he had leaned down close and reached his hand around to the back of Elena's head to grab a fist full of her long bleached blonde hair. As he gripped it reaching the end of his sentence. Elena reacted. 

A stray cat grabbed by the scruff of the neck is probably the best visual for what happened next. Elena literally tried to bite him as both of her hands with their long-manicured nail went to scratch his face and eyes. Theres all kinds of supers with all kinds of powers. Spotting a super is easy, they want people to know what they are, most of them are in the news on a regular basis for good or ill. But theirs never really a way to know all their powers. From his reaction and what happened next Ethan could guess at least two. First was some sort of invulnerability because neither the bite or the claws did anything to the super. But it did cause him to jerk back. That’s when Ethan figured out his second power. Strength, he pulled back and the next moment he was holding the women off the ground with one hand by the hair.

Shacking her he ordered "Shut UP"

If quieting her down was his intention it wasn't helping. Whether she realized what was going on or not if she went crazy before she bought the whole asylum now. She was screaming and thrashing it was like watching a parent hold a toddler while they threw a fit. She clawed at his hand and beat on his forearm but it had less effect than a toddler would.

Finely exasperated the super told her, "Look women shut your mouth or we'll find something to stick in it."

Ethan wasn't certain if he had meant to make an inuendo but obviously that was how Elena took it. Because she started kicking. As Ethan watched she must have kicked the super in the nuts four or five times. She just kept flutter kicking his ball sack.

Finally, Ethan saw the super react it was more frustration and irritation than anything else. He slammed the women head first into the table. It could have been her nose, her head or the table but Ethan heard a crack that made him cringe. Then for the first time since the two sat down everything in the bar went silent. 

Elena was moving trying to push herself up from the table but she was obviously to dazed to do it even if the super hadn't kept his hand on her head.

Then the silence broke as David jumped up and started slapping the super. Literally slapping him and not a full body slap that he actually put any strength into. It was a of little slaps with almost no momentum. Like a teenage girl in a cat fight. If Ethan wasn't so scared it might cause the super to just give up and tear the whole bar down on their heads he might have laughed.

"Let her go, you can't do that to her. Don’t you know how important she is?" David squealed in a falsetto as he continued to slap over and over.

Ethan could have sworn he saw the supers’ eyes roll. 

"Damn, the brown nose can speak." the super said. "I figured you'd just sit there and snivel".

"Here brown nose let me help you out." With that statement the super reached over and with single yank tore away the woman’s dress slacks. It must have hurt Elena but she barely reacted just writhing on the table.

With a grunt the super said "Grandma panties, really" and with a quick tug he tore those off as well.

Despite all that was happening Ethan had to appreciate the view. She might have been in her forties but the plastic surgery had been worth it at least it was to him since he didn’t have to pay the bill. Her asse was perfect. Tight and cupped. As she writhed on the table Ethan briefly got a glimpse of a blond bush for just a moment. 

Then the super grabbed the man by the back of his head. "You've been trying to do it all night might as well" and then the super forcefully pushed David’s face right into the crack of Elan’s asse. 

Ethan realized both of the supers’ hands were occupied now. If there was any time to make a break for it now was the time. As soon as he grabbed David, Ethan started to move headed for the door. As he approached the table he couldn't help but look. It was like a car accident in slow motion he couldn't look away and he got an eye full. 

As the super’s interaction with David had progressed Elena had started to struggle a bit more. She had her arms back behind her head scratching at the hand holding her head. 

Watching as he approached, Ethan was surprised to see her nails sink into his skin. 

No one knew where supers came from or what caused them to develop. One theory was that it bloomed in stressful situations. This theory was based on the fact that most supers manifest their powers for the first time when stressed. The counter to the theory was that people rarely tried the impossible unless they are stressed. Wherever they came from Ethan watched in amazement as her nails sunk into the super’s flesh. 

Was he watching a new super emerge. Two supers in the same bar. Ethan needed to get out of here if a super battle broke out all the normals would be dead fast. 

Then he also saw David able to push back against the man’s hand. Pulling his nose out of the crack of his exec’s asse. 

All this Ethan noticed in passing as he headed for the door. 

By the time he was abreast of the table the super was struggling. Elena had clawed at the hand on her head her nails leaving deep bloody gouges in the man’s hand and arm. David had got up far enough to slap at the super’s face. This time though it was one hard slap David brought all the way up from the table. This time the results were different the supers head rocked back. With David no longer face first in her asse Elena started kicking again. So just as the super’s head rocked back Ethan saw Elena's spiked six-inch stiletto heal embed itself right through the thin dress pants the man wore straight into the rather impressive bulge that had until that moment been growing in his pants.

With a cringe of sympathy Ethan was past the trio and out the door.
