# Chapter 2: The Fastest Blush of the Day

No sooner had Ethan cleared the doorway than a suit nearly knocked him over from behind. Glancing over, Ethan realized it was the super. Or at least what he had thought was a super. Because a super with the strength that guy had displayed when he picked up Elena by the hair would not have bounced off and sprawled onto the sidewalk. With one hand, he was clutching his crotch while he tried to climb back to his feet with the other.

Ethan realized there was no way this was a super so now he wasn't certain what to do. Should he help him or walk away. As he contemplated this conundrum, a screaming bleached blonde came storming out and kicked the man in the midriff. Contrary to his initial impression, she did not appear to be an emerging super. Being kicked by a super would probably do damage. Its possible she just had indestructable finger nails. There where wierder skills. Instead, the ninety-pound personification of a Karen on crack had to kick him twice before he was knocked back to the ground. At which point, she brought her foot up to reach her hands and proceeded to try to remove the six-inch stiletto heel that had proved so effective in the bar.

It was Elena. The same Elena that had just been forcefully and publicly disrobed, or at least mostly. She was furious, gorgeous, and half-naked. Watching her ass bounce as she kicked Superwimp would have caught any guy's eye. When she brought her foot up to get her shoe off, there was no way not to notice just how stretched out she was. There are all kinds of quirks out there. Some are barely a line, while others have more minora than majora. This second category was where Elena was firmly positioned. Her labia must have stuck out a good two inches. It was immediately obvious how she had gotten her executive position.

As she finally got her shoe off to presumably spike the man to death, David came running out with a large wade of black cloth that he was presumably planning on wrapping around her. Of course, in doing this, he nearly tackled her, and given she hadn’t quite gotten her foot down, all three ended up in a heap on the ground, giving Ethan another perfect view of some plastic surgeon’s masterpiece.

With the same nasal, petulant tone she had been using all night, she asked, "David, what are you doing?"

David, for his part, looked flabbergasted as he held up the remains of her pants.

"What is that?" Elena asked.

"It’s your slacks."

There was a moment where everyone froze. Elena, as she processed that realization, and the rest of everyone on the sidewalk watched her process that information. Her face had been red with rage as she came storming out the door to kick Superwimp; it went white. Of course, then, as her eyes met Ethan’s, along with everyone else gaping at her, quietly watching the show, she then got the November 4th, 2074 award for the fastest blush of the day. With a squeak of alarm, she grabbed the torn slacks from his hands and, covering her front with the bundle of cloth while making a futile attempt to cover her prodigious and bouncy derriere with the other hand, she ran back into the bar.

David, shooting a scowl at Superwimp and what seemed an even worse scowl at Ethan, ran in behind her, trying to help cover her plastic investments as they both headed into the girl’s bathroom together.

As sirens sounded in the distance, Ethan figured he had better move. Superwimp seemed to be alive, and the Elena and David duo would be sure to spin some bullshit to the cops about him if he was still present when they emerged. Walking briskly back down the street, Ethan glanced back just as he rounded the corner. Superwimp seemed to be standing up and walking the other way just fine. If Ethan wasn’t sure, but he appeared to be adjusting his junk as he ducked into an alley just down from the bar.

Ethan walked another half block before he realized he was actually going the wrong way. Bringing a map up on his Lens, it was trying to route him back past the bar. Instead, Ethan took a right and walked down a few blocks before the augmented reality navigation display had him turning right again instead of the persistent prompts to turn around.

As Ethan walked, he went back over what he had seen. It was a fairly common enough thing right up until the blonde went feral. Supers threw their weight around, and that guy was trying to do just that. Whether or not he was a super, if he could pull it off, no one would question him. Of course, in this case, he couldn't, so there were likely to be repercussions. Ethan didn’t think the guy was planning on it going as far as it did. It just kept escalating, so he kept flexing.

The last couple of years had been a mess. People started to get skills. Powers might be a better word, but the government had decided to call them skills. They started categorizing skills, debating skills, and studying skills. The media finally gave up and started calling them skills too. Strength was a common one, but there were a variety of variances. Muscle strength was different than tendon strength. Fast-twitch strength and slow-twitch strength. That guy was displaying slow-twitch strength. The problem with strength is there was always a weak point. It doesn't matter if your muscles can lift if your bones and tendons give out. With infinite muscle and tendon strength, you can only lift about six tons before your bones give out. If you had indestructible bones, then you might be able to pick up more.

Indestructible was another skill, but again it came in all sorts of flavors: skin, bones, brain, tendons, and so far they have cataloged an indestructible heart, liver, and gallbladder. That last one was a real surprise when they went to try and remove it. Wouldn’t have been so bad if it didn’t also come with indestructible gallstones. They seemed to find some new variant every year. The poor girl with indestructible toenails was having a real time of it.

That, of course, isn’t it. There is flight and telepathy and metal control and just about anything one could think of from every comic book, fantasy, RPG thing one might imagine, but none of them worked like everyone thought. For example, flight. The skills seem to lock onto either an organ or an organ system. If your skeletal system can fly, you are golden; it would pretty much work like everyone expects. But that almost never happens. You might just have flying eyeballs, which are pretty useless more than once. There are some claims that everyone has a skill; it's just no one ever tries to check the strength of their pancreas or try to make their ovaries fly, so they never find out.

The other thing no one expected was that skills don’t stop. You can't turn them on and off. Which is what made that guy in the bar so odd. If he was slow-twitch strong with indestructible skin the way he looked to start with, there’s no way for him to lose it or become as weak as he was at the end. He might have been jacked up on an adrenal to display strength, but there’s no way his skin would have taken those nails without indestructible skin. But then it was just gone. Too weak to hold Elena down or keep David brown-nosing. Something was off in that bar.

While Ethan took his round about trip to avoid the bar FireMan walked around the corner to get away from that bar. He wasn't certain what had just happened, but something had neutralized his skills. Everything had been fine till he touched that brown-noser, David. He shoved his face into the ass of his boss and suddenly he felt weaker than he ever had. He had never had issues making norms do what he wanted. But the bloody scratches on his arm and the pain in his groin were enough to let him know he wanted nothing to do with whatever just happened. Finding another haunt somewhere else in this city seemed like a good idea. Maybe move the penthouse; he was tired of this one anyway.
