# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter effectively executes the inciting incident, maintaining Ethan's limited perspective and the physical reality of the fall. However, it contains a significant continuity error regarding the paramedics' knowledge of Aurora's specific power set, which contradicts the established worldbuilding where skills are private and unverified. Additionally, the narrative slightly overstates Ethan's realization of his own role, attributing a level of causal certainty to him that the evidence in the scene does not support, potentially undermining the mystery element intended for the arc.

## Findings

- [Canon] The paramedics explicitly identify Aurora's lost power as 'invulnerability' and express surprise at her lack of it, implying prior knowledge of her specific skill set. In this world, skills are private, organ-locked, and not publicly verified; civilians and first responders should not know the specific nature of a super's powers unless it is common knowledge, which contradicts the premise that skills are hidden or unverified.
  Evidence: "Her invulnerability is gone," the first medic said... "I expected the skin to be... different."
- [Character] Ethan concludes with high certainty that he is the cause of the fall ('He had been the cause'), despite having no evidence linking his presence to the power loss. This contradicts his established characterization as a cautious, rational civilian who previously dismissed the bar incident as an anomaly. He should remain in a state of suspicion or fear, not confirmed guilt, as the mechanism is unknown to him.
  Evidence: He had been standing there. He had been the cause.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter resolves the immediate physical event but fails to establish the necessary ambiguity for the investigation arc. By having Ethan explicitly suspect himself as the cause, it prematurely resolves the 'unknown cause' mystery that is supposed to drive Aurora's investigation. The arc requires the cause to remain unknown to both parties, not just unexplained to the public.
  Evidence: What if he was the reason she fell? ... He had been the cause.
- [PhysicalLogic] The distance between Ethan and Aurora's impact site is physically impossible given the venue's layout and the crowd dynamics described.

- The chapter maintains high tension and effectively conveys the shock of the event.
- The internal monologue effectively captures Ethan's fear and isolation.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the paramedics' explicit reference to 'invulnerability' and their surprise at her fragility; have them treat her as a standard trauma case without assuming knowledge of her powers.
- Adjust Ethan's internal monologue to reflect suspicion and fear rather than certainty; he should wonder if he is connected, not know he is the cause.
- Ensure the mystery of the power loss remains unresolved for both Ethan and the public, preserving the setup for Aurora's investigation.
- Reconcile the spatial distance: If Ethan is in the crowd behind barriers, he cannot be 3-4m from the stage impact.
