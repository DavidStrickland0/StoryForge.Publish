## Engagement Findings

- The chapter fails to provide meaningful progression, instead replaying the immediate aftermath of the previous chapter's climax.
- The tone shifts abruptly to crude voyeurism, undermining reader investment in the characters.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the re-enactment of the escape and focus on the immediate reactions of the characters post-incident.
- Correct the super's physical state to reflect the injuries sustained in Chapter 1.
- Eliminate all explicit, non-consensual descriptions of the victim's body and genitalia.
- Align Ethan's internal monologue with his established cautious, ordinary perspective.
- Remove the conflicting 'skills' mechanical explanation and rely on the established canon of powers.
- Vary sentence structure and avoid habitual contrast reframing to improve prose quality.
- Provide a clear and plausible explanation for the super's sudden loss of strength and invulnerability, such as a specific trigger or mechanism that causes his powers to fail.
- Ensure that the physical interactions between the super and Elena are consistent with the super's previously demonstrated abilities and the established rules of his powers.
- Consider revising the scene to show the super's powers failing in a way that is consistent with his abilities and the narrative context, rather than suddenly ceasing without explanation.
