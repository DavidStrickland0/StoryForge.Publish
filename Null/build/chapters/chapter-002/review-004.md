# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter fails to maintain the required first-person POV, shifting to third-person narration and then to the antagonist's perspective. It contains severe continuity errors regarding the super's powers and physical state, contradicts the established canon of the bar incident, and violates writing rules regarding contrast reframing and prose style.

## Findings

- [POV] The narrative shifts from first-person (implied by context) to third-person limited, violating the required POV.
  Evidence: No sooner has Ethan clears the doorway then a suit nearly knocks him over from behind.
- [POV] The chapter ends with a head-hop into the antagonist's perspective, breaking the single viewpoint rule.
  Evidence: FireMan walked around the corner to get away from that bar. He wasn't certain what had just happened...
- [Continuity] The super is described as losing strength and invulnerability, contradicting the canon that he retained these powers during the assault in Chapter 1.
  Evidence: Because a super with the strength that guy displayed when he picked up Elena by the hair would not have bounced off and sprawled onto the sidewalk.
- [Continuity] The super is described as being kicked and injured, contradicting the established canon that he was invulnerable to Elena's attacks in Chapter 1.
  Evidence: A screaming bleached blonde came storming out and kicked the man in the midriff. Contrary to his initial impression she was no emerging super.
- [Continuity] The super is described as being able to walk away fine, contradicting the canon that he was left powerless and vulnerable in Chapter 1.
  Evidence: Superwimp seemed to be standing up and walking the other way just fine.
- [Style] The chapter uses habitual contrast refring, violating the writing rule against defining images by denying one interpretation and replacing it with another.
  Evidence: Contrary to his initial impression she was no emerging super. Because being kicked by a super like that would send a man flying.
- [Style] The chapter contains excessive and explicit sexual description that is not appropriate for the tone and violates the rule against forcing sexual content into scenes where it has no narrative purpose.
  Evidence: Here Labia must have stuck out a good two inches. It was immediately obvious how she had gotten her executive position.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the bar incident's aftermath without advancing the narrative, violating the rule against re-performing completed narrative work.
  Evidence: As he contemplated this conundrum. A screaming bleached blonde came storming out and kicked the man in the midriff.
- [Character] Ethan's reaction to the super's injury is inconsistent with his established caution and fear of supers in Chapter 1.
  Evidence: Ethan wasn't certain if he should help him or walk away.
- [World] The chapter introduces a new power system (skills) that contradicts the established canon of supers having fixed powers.
  Evidence: The last couple of years had been a mess. People started to get skills. Powers might be a better word, but the government had decided to call them skills.
- [World] The chapter describes powers as being able to be turned on and off, contradicting the canon that powers are constant.
  Evidence: The other thing no one expected was that skills don’t stop. You can't turn them on and off.
- [World] The chapter describes powers as being able to be lost, contradicting the canon that powers are permanent.
  Evidence: If he was slow twitch strong with indestructible skin the way he looked to start with there’s no way for him to lose it or become as weak as he was at the end.
- [PhysicalLogic] The super's sudden loss of strength is physically inconsistent with his established invulnerability and the mechanics of the scene. The text states he had 'indestructible skin' and 'slow twitch strength' which made him immune to Elena's claws and David's slaps. However, when Ethan passes, the super immediately becomes weak enough to be kicked to the ground and struggle to stand. This contradicts the premise that his powers (specifically invulnerability and strength) are linked and cannot be selectively removed by proximity alone without a specific mechanism, and it ignores the fact that if his strength vanished, his invulnerability should also vanish, making him vulnerable to the very attacks that previously had no effect. The text explicitly notes 'there's no way for him to lose it or become as weak as he was at the end' if he had indestructible skin, yet he does.
  Evidence: Because a super with the strength that guy displayed when he picked up Elena by the hair would not have bounced off and sprawled onto the sidewalk... If he was slow twitch strong with indestructible skin the way he looked to start with there’s no way for him to lose it or become as weak as he was at the end.
- [PhysicalLogic] The sequence of Ethan exiting the bar and the super's reaction is spatially and temporally incoherent. Ethan clears the doorway and is nearly knocked over by the super from behind. This implies the super was behind Ethan at the door. However, the super was previously described as struggling with Elena and David at the table inside the bar. For the super to be behind Ethan at the door, he would have had to move from the table to the door, pass Ethan, and then be behind him, all while being described as 'struggling' and 'weak'. Furthermore, the text says the super 'bounced off and sprawled onto the sidewalk' after being kicked by Elena, but then later says he was 'standing up and walking the other way just fine' after Ethan rounded the corner. The transition from being kicked to the ground to standing and walking away is not described and contradicts the immediate aftermath of the kick.
  Evidence: No sooner has Ethan clears the doorway then a suit nearly knocks him over from behind... A screaming bleached blonde came storming out and kicked the man in the midriff... Superwimp seemed to be standing up and walking the other way just fine.
- [PhysicalLogic] Elena's action of kicking the super in the midriff twice to knock him to the ground is physically implausible given the super's established strength and the fact that he was previously holding Elena off the ground with one hand. The text states that 'being kicked by a super like that would send a man flying,' implying the super is strong. However, Elena, described as a 'ninety pound personification of a Karen,' is able to kick him twice and knock him to the ground. This contradicts the earlier description of the super's strength and the fact that he was able to hold Elena down and push David's face into her. The sudden loss of strength is not explained by a physical mechanism that would allow a 90-pound woman to knock out a man who was previously overpowering her and David.
  Evidence: A screaming bleached blonde came storming out and kicked the man in the midriff... the ninety pound personification of a Karen on crack had to kick him twice before he was knocked back to the ground.
- [PhysicalLogic] The physical interaction between David and Elena when David tries to cover her with her pants is spatially incoherent. The text states that David 'nearly tackled her' and 'given she hadn’t quite gotten her foot down all three ended up in a heap on the ground.' This implies that David, Elena, and the super all fell to the ground together. However, the super was previously described as being kicked to the ground by Elena, and then later as standing up and walking away. The sequence of events does not support the idea that all three ended up in a heap on the ground, especially since the super was described as being weak and struggling, but not necessarily on the ground at the moment David approached. The text also says David 'came running out with what was left of her pants wrapping it around her,' which implies he was already outside, but then he 'nearly tackled her' and they all fell, which is a sudden and unexplained change in their positions.
  Evidence: As she finally got her shoe off to presumably spike the man to death David came running out with what was left of her pants wrapping it around her. Of course, in doing this he nearly tackled her and given she hadn’t quite gotten her foot down all three ended up in a heap on the ground
- [PhysicalLogic] The timeline of Ethan's exit and the subsequent actions of the other characters is inconsistent. Ethan clears the doorway and is nearly knocked over by the super. Then Elena kicks the super. Then David comes out with the pants. Then Elena and David run back into the bar. Then Ethan walks briskly down the street and rounds the corner. Then he sees the super standing up and walking away. This sequence implies that the super was on the sidewalk, then kicked, then standing up, then walking away, all while Ethan was still in the process of exiting and walking down the street. The text does not provide a clear timeline for when the super moved from the sidewalk to the alley, and it is unclear how he was able to stand up and walk away so quickly after being kicked to the ground and described as weak.
  Evidence: No sooner has Ethan clears the doorway then a suit nearly knocks him over from behind... A screaming bleached blonde came storming out and kicked the man in the midriff... Superwimp seemed to be standing up and walking the other way just fine... he appeared to be adjusting his junk as he ducked into an alley just down from the bar.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter lacks narrative progression and repeats the bar incident's aftermath.
- The chapter contains excessive and explicit sexual description that is not appropriate for the tone.

## Revision Guidance

- Rewrite the chapter in first-person POV, maintaining a single viewpoint.
- Remove the head-hop into the antagonist's perspective.
- Correct the continuity errors regarding the super's powers and physical state.
- Remove the habitual contrast refring and excessive sexual description.
- Advance the narrative by developing the immediate aftermath of the bar incident.
- Clarify the mechanism by which the super's strength and invulnerability are lost. If it is due to Ethan's proximity, explain how this affects his physical capabilities and why it allows Elena to kick him to the ground. Ensure that the loss of strength is consistent with the established rules of his powers.
- Reorder the sequence of events to make the spatial and temporal logic clear. For example, describe the super's position relative to Ethan and the door, and how he moves from the table to the door. Ensure that the actions of Elena, David, and the super are consistent with their positions and the timeline of the scene.
- Provide a more plausible explanation for how Elena is able to kick the super to the ground. If his strength is lost, explain how this affects his ability to resist her kicks. Ensure that the physical interaction between the characters is consistent with their established strengths and weaknesses.
- Clarify the sequence of events when David tries to cover Elena with her pants. Describe their positions and how they end up in a heap on the ground. Ensure that the actions of the characters are consistent with their positions and the timeline of the scene.
- Provide a clear timeline for the super's movements from the sidewalk to the alley. Describe how he moves from being kicked to the ground to standing up and walking away. Ensure that the sequence of events is consistent with the established rules of his powers and the physical reality of the scene.
