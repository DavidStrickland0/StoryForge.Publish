# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter fails to advance the narrative by re-describing the aftermath of the bar incident without resolving the immediate physical state of the characters or establishing the super's reaction to his power loss. It contains significant continuity errors regarding the super's location and physical condition, contradicts the established canon of the super's presence, and relies on repetitive, explanatory prose that violates writing rules against contrast reframing and unnecessary exposition. The chapter does not meet the Chapter 2 target of developing concrete responses to the incident.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the description of the super inside the laundromat window and establish his location and reaction to his power loss within the bar.
- Clarify that the super's power loss is temporary and proximity-dependent, and establish his immediate reaction to this loss.
- Develop the immediate aftermath of the incident by establishing concrete responses from the super, Elena, and David, and the consequences of the incident.
- Remove repetitive contrast reframing and unnecessary explanations of Ethan's emotional state and the super's confusion.
- Establish the super's identity and his next actions, creating a meaningful obstacle or discovery that advances the narrative.
- Clarify Ethan's physical position relative to the bar. If he is observing the super through a window, he must remain at the bar's entrance or a window facing the interior. If he is walking down the street, he cannot see inside the bar unless the bar has a large window facing the street and he is still directly in front of it. The mention of a 'laundromat' suggests a different location, which contradicts the sustained observation of the bar's interior.
- Ensure the spatial relationship between Ethan, the bar, and the laundromat is consistent. If the laundromat is next to the bar, specify that he is looking through the bar's window, not the laundromat's. If he has moved away, he should no longer be able to see the super inside.
