# Crucible of Ages — Book One Premise

## Working Title: The Weight of Chains

Caelen, a United States Marine, dies shielding a noncombatant mother from Sergeant Miller's gunfire. The mother survives. His last act gives him peace that his selfish and domineering choices did not.

At death, his hidden scales favor Dominion, but his rescue triggers Sacrifice assignment and the conflict grants him a rare choice. He quickly chooses Sacrifice without understanding its implications. The Creator visits to verify the choice and tells him Earth breeds candidates to rule the universe.

Caelen experiences time in Nothing as memory loses acuteness. His body is later reformed from dust on another planet. He is captured without local language, cultural knowledge, or recognized protection.

In the slave carriage he meets Marcy, an adult non-awakened woman sold after her indentured parents died owing manufactured debt. She desperately claims him as protector. His ignorance of the custom leads to fights on her behalf before he understands their cause.

Caelen is a Fighter using SRD 3.5 progression behind the scenes. Only Sacrifice-aligned actions advance him. Sacrifice means loving others above oneself and prioritizing their needs and wants over one's own. Dominion means loving oneself above others and prioritizing one's own wants and needs over theirs. Faction alignment depends on true motivation, which the System knows perfectly; apparent selflessness, personal cost or risk, and the outcome do not determine faction alignment. XP accrues only for actions aligned with the assigned faction. A 50/50 balance of motivations grants zero XP; 100/0 in favor of the assigned faction grants full eligible XP. Mixed motivations attenuate XP. No exact intermediate attenuation formula is established. The SRD rules continue to determine eligible experience valuation; these motivation rules govern eligibility and attenuation without replacing SRD valuation. He sees no XP or levels; advancement improves stats and opens learning potential, with skills acquired through prose. The author manages most mechanics manually.

The planet is fragmented among warlords and Mongol-style kings. Book One follows Caelen from slave through liberation, alliances, conflict, and unification to planetary rulership on the Sacrifice path. The question is whether he can govern without converting others' dependence into personal possession.

Throughout, odd information gives him critical clues. Only at the ending does he recognize the Creator's guidance. Once he becomes planetary ruler, he learns the hidden plan: 120,000 rulers capable of sacrifice and rule; only Sacrifice rulers are accepted, while Dominion rulers are destroyed and replaced.
