# Canon

## Established Canon


- Caelen is an eighteen-year-old United States Marine.


- Caelen died on Earth during his first life.


- Caelen died while shielding a noncombatant mother from Sergeant Miller's gunfire.


- The noncombatant mother was carrying the corpse of a dead infant.


- Caelen possesses the System interface, which displays two distinct columns of advancement levels.


- The System interface is private and cannot be seen or verified by others.


- Caelen is currently in the void, suspended between lives.


- Caelen's physical body was destroyed by gunfire, with wounds in his chest and thigh.


- Caelen's past actions included lying to his mother about injuries and taking credit for Private Davis's tactical call.


- Caelen's new body is physically distinct from his previous Marine body, featuring larger, broad-palmed hands and tanned, rough skin.


- The gravity on Caelen's new world is heavier than Earth's, or his body is unaccustomed to its own weight.


- Caelen's new world has a pale, washed-out yellow sky and a dim, hazy sun.


- Caelen does not speak or understand the local language of his new world.


- Caelen was fitted with a metal collar that locked around his neck.


- Caelen is currently enslaved and being treated as an asset or object of trade.

## Changes Over Time


- Caelen was alive and active in a combat zone; Caelen is now dead and in the void.


- Caelen was alive and physically present in a combat zone; Caelen is now dead and his consciousness is suspended in the void.


- Caelen did not possess the System; Caelen now possesses the System and can perceive his advancement levels.


- Caelen was dead and suspended in the void; Caelen is now alive and physically present in a new world.


- Caelen was in a formless void; Caelen is now in a physical environment with gravity, air, and sensory input.
