# Scene 6: Scene 6

The man in purple stopped tapping his cane.

He looked at the girl, then at Caelen, and spoke. The words came evenly, while the words where unfamiliar there seemed to be no anger in the tone or tamper, just a consistent voice with words evenly spaced.

Everyone else in the room heard the man in purple say, "New slaves have a week to adjust before they must meet their quota. He would have had that week."

The man in purple pointed his cane at Caelen while addressing the girl beside him.

"But he chose to shield you from your punishment. Seems he's ready now to start acting. His quota begins tomorrow. And you will meet it, as well as your own."

For a moment she did not seem to breathe. Her eyes moved from the cane to Caelen's face, then dropped to the floor. Her mouth opened, but whatever she had been about to say never came out.

Caelen looked between them. He had heard the change in her breathing. That, and the way she had drawn her elbows against her sides, told him something unpleasant had happened.

The man in purple lowered the cane. He was still watching her.

At last she nodded.

It was a small movement, scarcely more than a dip of her chin. Caelen waited for her to look up again, but she kept her eyes on the stone. He wanted to ask another question. Without a single shared word between them, he had no idea how to ask one she could answer.

Across the cell, somebody shifted. Cloth brushed stone. The small sounds of people trying not to attract attention began again.

The man in purple turned back to his ledger.

Caelen stayed beside her. Nothing had touched her since the guard had withdrawn, yet she looked more frightened now than when Caelen had helped her stand.

Caelen was trying to get his head around the situation. He hadn't really thought much since he had arrived in this strange new world. He'd just been reacting. 

As he looked around the cell, the first thing he noticed was that there was some sort of meeting going on. Mr. Purple seemed to continue. He'd call out three words. Followed by a pause.

"Kael Varen    tha"
"Zane Korin    tha"
"Taren Voss    na"
After the third repetition, Scarface picked up a cane and approached an old man that immediately started begging. Scarface didn't pay any attention and just started beating on him. While that happened, Mr.  Purple continued.
"Rylan Zor    da" 
This time the man that had caned Caelen stepped over to another man and likewise started beating him. 

Caelen's stomach lurched watching the brutality. Something deep inside screamed at Caelen to move to help these men. Caelen had actually been surprised that he had moved so quickly to help the women beside him, barely awake he hadn't thought, he just followed instincts to protect her. The same instincts that were prompting him now to again move and protect but he had better control over it now. 
Mr. Purple continued 
"Kira Venn    tha"
"Dax Maren    tha"
"Vela Torin    cha"
Scarface left off his caning of the old man to approach a middle aged woman with an arm in a sling. Her face was hard and emotionless as he approached. As he got within reach she just crouched down and protected her arm with her body, presenting her back to the cane.
Again Caelen felt his instincts screaming at him to protect her. To stand between the women and the cane. It was odd, long ago he had felt the desire to protect an attractive women or two. Stepping in at a bar helping a bouncer etc. This was different though. The women wasn't really all that attractive and he even felt some push to protect the men that had gotten beaten. 
"Zarek Vale    tha"
"Nira Kalen    tha"
"Joren Tal     tha"

Everyone just stood and watched as the men beat the others. It was then Caelen realized that everyone wore a collar, both the beaten and the two slabs of muscle. The only person that didn't seem to have a collar on was the man in purple that seemed to be reading from table top.
There was another oddity. The room was crowded but no one but the girl and one other stood within ten cubits of him. He had first thought it was a by product of the beating. That they had given them space so they didn't get hit. But now people continued to crowd fairly close to the swinging cane, choosing to risk being hit rather then get any closer to them.
After another few lines and another beating was delivered, Mr. Purple stood and stepping around the table he walked toward the cell door with his two henchmen in tow. While the way to the door was clear, all three men seemed to walk around the group of three staying that same ten cubits away only walking through the outer edge of the open space. They gave no reaction or indication why they stayed away they just did.
Finally reaching the door they knocked on it. The guards unlocked the door and let them out.

After they left, Caelen tried to parse what just happened. Those had to be names followed by a status. 'Tha' was acceptable; anything else wasn't. It was some kind of ceremony or accounting, it might just be a random brutalization, but it was targeted at the weak and the injured: the small slip of a girl, the old man, the injured arm. Perhaps it was performance-based.

Glancing around the room, Caelen's gaze fell on the girl. She had moved away from him, and as his gaze fell upon her, obvious fear fell on her face and she physically flinched. She took one step back, then a second, causing her to press up against the wall. With that action, she flinched again, rebounding away from the wall.

Caelen remembered her injury—the one strike she had taken before he got to her. He realized she was probably the first name on the list and calling her out had awakened him.

Coming back to current events, Caelen realized he had been staring at her while he thought, and she had shriveled in fear, crouching into the corner. Even as she crouched though, she was at an awkward angle, pressing her butt into the corner while keeping her back off the stone. Realizing that one strike must have done a lot of damage, he took a step forward.

"Sorry, let's take a look. I'm not sure what we can do, but at least some pressure will stop any bleeding," Caelen told her.

The fearful look and the wince of pain as she pressed her back against the wall reminded Caelen that she didn't understand him.

So, on the fly, he came up with a way to express what he wanted. He mimed pulling up his shirt and then pointed at his eyes and at her. It likely wasn't the best charade, but she should get the point.

As he took a step forward, her eyes grew big, and then she obviously got angry as she reached for the edge of her shirt and began to drag it up.

Realizing she was about to remove her shirt in a room mostly full of men, Caelen rushed forward to stop her.

Seeing him rushing forward, she let out a smothered "eep," but fortunately, she stopped long enough for Caelen to grab her hand. With a twirling motion, he told her to turn around. Once she was facing the corner, Caelen lifted up the back of her shirt. There was a red patch with several other spots in a line across her blouse. Caelen had intended to just lift her shirt enough to see the spot she had been hit, but as he lifted it, it revealed a bruised tapestry of purpling skin and welts. While he finally did reach today's bleeding line, he continued to pull until her entire back was one solid bruise with purple skin and healing cuts that must hurt every time she moved.

The worst spot—the spot from today—was in the middle of a puffy, infected area with blood and pus oozing from where the cane had reopened an old wound. Reaching out, Caelen gently pressed the palm of his hand against her back in an attempt to stop the bleeding.

Her skin was hot; inflamed, the infection was already setting in with red lines of blood poisoning spiderwebbing across her back, highlighting the infected veins. A memory came of sepsis—the sweating and shivering he had taken as fear took on a new light.

There were no bandages or salves.

Caelen desperately wanted to help her. Those new instincts cried out to protect her, and in that moment, something went out from him. Some of his strength, or maybe some of his energy—whatever it was—the spiderweb of veins disappeared. The purple and welts faded; they didn't disappear, they just faded. A moan of relief came from the girl as the pain lessened. Caelen felt her relax under his hand, but then all that tension came right back as he heard a voice from behind him not knowing exactly what was said. Caelen turned to see what he could only assume was the B.B.B., the biggest badest bitch in the room. There was always one in any group and in a co-ed cell it would revert to the strongest god serviced by the weakest.

Caelen let out a sigh. He had almost known this was coming. It was too cliché not to. Small slip of a girl in a co-ed cell—what else was going to happen—and from the looks of her back, she had been here a while.
