## Engagement Findings

- The chapter lacks a clear narrative progression, failing to provide a new obstacle or development that moves the story forward.
- The prose is repetitive and relies on abstract explanations rather than concrete actions and consequences.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the verbatim repetition of the previous chapter's ending and create a new, distinct closing beat.
- Develop the labor-for-water arrangement by exploring its consequences and how it changes Caelen's position.
- Reduce the use of clipped sentences and contrast reframing, allowing the actions to speak for themselves.
- Show the bond between Caelen and the boy through their interactions, rather than treating them as separate entities.
