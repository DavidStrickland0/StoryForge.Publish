# Chapter 4: The Asset

The cart rattled over a cobblestone street that seemed to have been paved by someone with a grudge. Every bump jolted Caelen’s bound wrists, the metal cuffs biting into his skin with a rhythmic, metallic pain. The dust from the road had settled into his pores, coating his tongue with grit that tasted of iron and dry earth. He stumbled, his legs shaking from the suspension of his arms, but he caught his balance. He did not fall. He had trained too long to fall on command.
