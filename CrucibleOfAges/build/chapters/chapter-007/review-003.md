## Engagement Findings

- The chapter lacks forward momentum, spending its entire length re-enacting previous scenes rather than introducing new conflicts, obligations, or character developments required by the active Short arc.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the repeated water refusal and protection pledge scenes; assume these events have already occurred and focus on their consequences.
- Advance the narrative to the next milestone: introduce a new daily obligation or complication arising from the established labor arrangement.
- Eliminate the 'not X, but Y' contrast reframing patterns; state observations directly and positively to improve prose style.
- Ensure the chapter progresses from the state established in Chapter 6, rather than resetting to the state of Chapter 5.
- Remove the re-enacted scene of the boy offering water from his pot, as this event has already occurred and the pot is established to be empty.
- Correct the continuity regarding the canteen: Caelen already possesses it, so he should not be receiving it from a guard and handing it back.
- Resolve the physical implausibility of the stone pile doubling overnight by either removing the specific count or providing a plausible explanation for the accumulation of materials.
- Adjust the description of Caelen's canteen to reflect that he has just received it and has not yet had the opportunity to ration it over a night.
