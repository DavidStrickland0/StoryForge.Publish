## Revision Guidance

- Remove the verbatim copy of the previous chapter's ending and replace it with a new, distinct conclusion that reflects the day's events.
- Introduce a new daily obligation or consequence that advances the Short arc, such as a penalty for failure or a new demand from the overseer.
- Vary sentence structure and avoid clipped fragments; develop thoughts naturally instead of stacking short statements.
- Reduce the use of contrast reframing ('not X, but Y') and state observations directly and positively.
- Explain the origin or significance of the clay bricks to maintain continuity and provide context for the new task.
