# Characters

## Continuity Basis

Current state is anchored to the end of accepted Chapter 4. Earlier states belong in history. Descriptive identifiers remain in use until names are learned in the story. Caelen's readings of motives and rank remain interpretations unless independently established.

## Caelen

### Established History

- A United States Marine in his first life on Earth.
- Sought status, escape, belonging, and significance; capable of courage and loyalty alongside selfishness and exploitation.
- Lied to his mother about injuries and took credit for Private Davis's tactical call.
- Died shielding a noncombatant mother carrying her dead infant from Sergeant Miller's gunfire. The mother survived.
- He truly died on Earth; his Earth body was destroyed. He remained dead in Nothing (the void), experiencing the passage of time while the acuteness of his memories gradually diminished. Later, his body was reformed from local dust on another world, where he awakened alive.
- He is an awakened person from Earth and perceives a private System. Everyone from Earth eventually awakens. His intentional rescue death triggers automatic Sacrifice assignment. His tutorial choices weigh so strongly toward Dominion that this conflict triggers a rare offer to choose between factions. At death he quickly chooses Sacrifice, without understanding its mechanics. He dislikes what he became on Earth and finds peace only in his final rescue. This weighting reflects hidden faction points, inaccessible during life and weighed only at death.

### Current State

- Alive, collared, and captive in the slave pen at the end of Chapter 4. His declaration of identity is resolve; he has not escaped or obtained legal freedom.
- The returned body has larger, broad-palmed hands and tanned, rough skin. It is locally adapted and physically conditioned but initially unfamiliar.
- Sustained a staff blow to the ribs during capture. Recovery has not been established. Transport restraints injured his wrists; their exact configuration after entering the pen should not be assumed without clarification in prose.
- Cannot understand the local language. Recognizes some situational cues, which do not grant precise knowledge of laws or motives.
- Sees two private advancement columns showing one and one. Does not understand their meaning. No level increase, class selection, or usable ability has been demonstrated.
- Bluffed a guard into lowering his staff and has attracted attention from guards and captives. His standing is provisional.
- Immediate aims are survival, understanding the local rules, and eventual freedom. His latest tactic secured personal position rather than establishing a selfless commitment to the other captives.

### Knowledge from the Creator's Visit

After Caelen chooses Sacrifice at death, the Creator personally visits him to verify his choice. Caelen learns that Earth is the breeding ground of candidates to rule the Creator's universe. He does not learn that only Sacrifice rulers are accepted or that Dominion rulers are destroyed and replaced. The target number of 120,000, the Sacrifice-only acceptance rule, and the destruction and replacement of Dominion rulers are withheld from him and the reader until the end.

The visit does not grant a full understanding of faction advancement or the interface. His later doubts remain possible despite this encounter.

### Faction Choice and Later Doubts

Caelen chose Sacrifice quickly at death. He knew he disliked the person he had become and that his final rescue gave him peace; he did not understand the faction's advancement rules. Remembering or choosing the name does not mean he understands the unexplained interface.

Later he notices that his growth seems connected to sacrifice or genuinely accepted potential sacrifice for others, including defending them with his life and sword, without understanding the hidden progression mechanism. He occasionally questions his choice when this restriction frustrates him. Those doubts coexist with his capacity to care and his continuing selfish impulses; they do not change his faction during life.

### Writing Guidance

His trained habits support observation, endurance, and threat assessment. His experience and judgment have limits independent of any specified age. His extensive military hand-to-hand training is established. Do not invent a service history or command experience merely to explain a convenient capability.

He may mistake hierarchy for stability, compliance for agreement, or usefulness for loyalty. Let those mistakes meet specific people and consequences. His early change should emerge through costly exceptions to self-interest while Dominion methods remain tempting and effective.

Preserve a recognizable voice: practical attention, defensive pride, impatience with helplessness, and dry humor when circumstances permit. Allow fear, embarrassment, grief, tenderness, and dependence to complicate that voice.

## Supporting Character Development

For recurring characters, establish a private goal, useful competence, dependency, boundary, and relationship beyond Caelen. Reveal these through interaction. Captives may disagree about acceptable risk, protect people he does not know, or reject an escape proposal for credible reasons.

Track changes in trust separately from obedience and gratitude. A person can accept help while refusing recruitment; a rival can cooperate without becoming a friend. Preserve distinct motives instead of assigning everyone the same fear or resentment.

## The Creator

- Created the universe and seeks 120,000 rulers capable of both sacrifice and rule.
- Established Earth as the breeding ground of prospective rulers.
- Makes a rare visit to Caelen after his faction choice to verify it and reveals the search for rulers.
- Does not reveal that only Sacrifice rulers are accepted, or that Dominion rulers are destroyed and replaced by new candidates.
- Exact selection tests, destruction mechanism, and replacement procedure remain undefined.

## End Revelation — Planetary Rulership

The revelation occurs once Caelen becomes ruler of his planet, as one of the few Sacrifice rulers. At that point he learns that the Creator seeks 120,000 rulers capable of both sacrifice and rule, accepts only Sacrifice rulers, and destroys Dominion rulers while putting forth new candidates.

This is the culmination of Caelen's planetary rulership arc, not the earlier liberation of one slave operation or the formation of a small army. Keep these selection truths hidden from Caelen and the reader until he reaches that milestone. Do not treat his early choice or the Creator's verification visit as proof that he has already achieved planetary rulership or passed the final selection.

### The Creator's Continuing Interest

The Creator takes a unique interest in Caelen and prompts him through apparently random data delivered in odd ways. These provide critical clues without intervention in events. Caelen must interpret and act; Caelen does not recognize the source until the ending. The reason for the Creator's special interest and the specific delivery mechanisms remain undefined. This guidance preserves the secrecy of the final selection revelation.

## Retrospective Recognition of Guidance

Only at the ending, once he becomes ruler of his planet, does Caelen look back and recognize that the odd information and critical clues were supplied by the Creator. Earlier, he can understand and use a clue without knowing its source. His early verification visit does not make him recognize the later prompts as divine guidance.

Prepare this recognition through concrete, memorable clues whose connections become clear in retrospect. Before the ending, preserve Caelen's unawareness; do not give him confirmed knowledge of the Creator's ongoing involvement. The final recognition connects earlier events without erasing his choices, work, sacrifices, or responsibility.

## Caelen's Fighter Progression — Hidden Framework

Caelen is a Fighter. The D&D SRD 3.5 Fighter provides the behind-the-scenes progression framework for his combat capabilities, statistics, proficiencies, skills, feats, and learning prerequisites. This framework does not appear in the fiction as a visible class-level or XP system.

There are no displayed XP gains, XP totals, character levels, or level-up announcements. Caelen does not know that Sacrifice-aligned actions govern his advancement. Do not have him calculate XP, identify his numerical level, or reliably explain the hidden advancement mechanism.

Only Sacrifice-aligned actions advance him, including actual sacrifice or genuine potential sacrifice for others. Combat, kills, victory, practice, and completed objectives do not independently trigger advancement. Practice develops what he is capable of learning; it is not a substitute for the hidden Sacrifice requirement.

When he crosses a hidden advancement threshold, his stats improve and new learning potential opens. Apply the established Fighter progression behind the scenes rather than inventing unrelated class powers. Do not automatically give him a finished technique, complete knowledge, or mastery merely because that potential becomes available.

Learning occurs in the prose through instruction, observation, experimentation, training, mistakes, and use under pressure. New skills and techniques require an earned development sequence. A new capacity may first appear as improved coordination, endurance, understanding, or ability to benefit from instruction, as appropriate to the underlying change.

Keep capability growth distinct from hidden faction scales. Neither improved performance nor any interface reveals where his Dominion and Sacrifice scales lie during life. He may notice patterns or wonder about his growth, but narration must not turn that uncertainty into knowledge of XP or levels.

Exact current statistics, learned techniques, advancement thresholds, and the presentation of stats remain undefined. The two displayed ones are not confirmed XP, levels, or faction-scale readings. Military experience informs behavior without granting unlimited competence.

## Marcy — Core Companion and Love Interest

### Background

Marcy is a young, non-awakened woman and Caelen's female love interest. She is an adult; do not state her numerical age. Chronic malnutrition has left her small-framed, underweight, and physically underdeveloped for her age. Describe this through health, strength, clothing fit, fatigue, and recovery; do not mistake her youthful appearance for her actual age. Her parents were indentured servants paid wages while being charged for necessities and other expenses in amounts designed to exceed their earnings. The resulting debt could not be paid off. After both parents died, Marcy was sold into slavery to pay their supposed outstanding debt.

### First Meeting and Protector Claim

Caelen meets Marcy in the slave carriage during the opening chapters. Her beauty draws the overseer's attention. Other men in the slave cage harass her or attempt to sexually assault her; Caelen is the only man there who does not join them. His lack of participation is initially rooted largely in his inability to understand the language and recognize what is happening. Do not rewrite that passivity as a fully informed heroic stand.

His different behavior makes Marcy trust him enough to claim him as her protector in desperation. Under slave-pit custom, she claims to have given her life to him in exchange for protection, becoming subordinate to him during her free time. Caelen does not understand the declaration or the obligations it creates.

Because he neither answers the claim nor surrenders her, other slaves force him to fight over her. His extensive military hand-to-hand training helps him prevail. He eventually learns enough language to understand why these confrontations have been happening and what Marcy has claimed.

### Relationship Development

Her initial trust is a desperate judgment about relative safety, not established romantic love or sexual consent. The protector claim does not constitute consent to intimacy. Let romance develop through mutual understanding, choices, and accumulated experience after adult status is established.

Marcy's dependence confronts Caelen with a concrete form of the story's central tension: protecting someone can place him in a position to possess or exploit her. Her claim is a survival action under coercive conditions, not evidence that she freely endorses slavery or wants to surrender all future autonomy.

Her personality, skills, private ambitions, grief, and relationships beyond Caelen remain to be developed. Do not reduce her role to beauty, victimization, gratitude, or motivation for his fights.

### Author-Established Progression in This Episode

Caelen earns several hidden Fighter advancement steps through the fights on Marcy's behalf. These are author-established outcomes, not visible levels or XP rewards. Exact counts and stat changes will be supplied manually. New learning potential opens, while actual acquisition of skills and techniques develops through prose. Do not award advancement merely for a win; preserve the established Sacrifice requirement and show the personal risk borne for Marcy, without requiring Caelen to understand the faction mechanics or custom.

### Opening-Chapter Integration

The carriage meeting and protector claim are author-established opening material that must be integrated into the existing chapters. Do not silently assert that the currently accepted text already depicts them. Whether Marcy is the distressed young woman in Chapter 4 remains unestablished; keep their identities separate unless deliberately connected.

## Age References in Prose

Do not state anyone's numerical age or a body's numerical age in the prose. Convey adult status, physical maturity, health, and experience through characterization and description. Avoid Earth-year comparisons, including describing Marcy as having the body of a particular younger age. Her adult status remains established. There are no seasons, years, or calendar. Elapsed days are tracked in periods of eight, thirty-two, and one hundred twenty-eight days.

## Caelen's Hands — Visible Difference

Caelen has five digits on each hand, including the thumb. Local people normally have three fingers and a thumb on each hand and four toes per foot, so his hands are visibly abnormal to them. Preserve this feature alongside his larger, broad-palmed hands. His own toe count remains unspecified. The difference can draw attention without establishing that observers recognize him as awakened.

## Marcy's Age Knowledge and Caelen's Early Unease

Marcy's adult status remains established. Her malnutrition and younger appearance contribute to Caelen's early struggle with his attraction to her. He lacks familiar cultural and chronological cues and must learn about her through conversation rather than assuming appearance or local custom establishes maturity.

Marcy does not routinely track her age after naming day. It takes persistent questioning for Caelen to draw out what she knows about her age and history. Do not make her supply an exact Earth-year age or a conveniently precise uninterrupted day count without an established basis. Her memories and understanding of life stages can emerge gradually.

Caelen's attraction and uncertainty are part of the early premise. Develop them through restraint, conversation, concern, and increased understanding of Marcy as a person. The protector claim, absence of local age restrictions, and naming day do not constitute consent or establish adulthood. Her dependence on him remains relevant to their developing relationship.
