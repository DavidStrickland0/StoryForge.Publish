Caelen opened his eyes. Pale yellow light made him squint. He turned his face aside and found bare ground inches from his cheek. He tried to draw a breath, half expecting it not to work and half expecting pain in his chest for some reason. But air rushed in, filling lungs that felt heavy and alien. There was no pain, only a profound disorientation, as if his body were a vessel he had never touched before.

Laying still he tried to remember where he was and how he had got there. He remembered a gunfight that seemed to be both recent and long ago.  There where faces, ideas, motivations. He remembered he was a Marine but then wasn't certain what that was. It all seemed both important and distant like remembering something very important that he had forgot years ago. 

He pushed himself up with trembling arms, muscles straining against a sensation of unfamiliar density. His palms scraped across grit and hard-packed earth. The texture felt coarse beneath palms that were somehow different then he expected. Larger and broader than he expected for some reason. He looked down at them, flexing fingers that seemed built for digging rather than holding a rifle. His skin was tanned and rough, adapted to a sun that hung low and dim in the pale sky above. Was this gravity heavier? Every movement required more effort than he could recall from his long-ago life as a Marine.

“Where…?” His voice came out hoarse. He swallowed and tried again, but the scrape of a boot made him stop.

A figure approached from the dust near a stone wall, half-hidden behind a low ridge of earth. A stranger moved with purpose, boots crunching on loose gravel. Caelen froze, heart hammering in a chest he couldn’t quite gauge. His instincts told him to take a ready stance. Was this someone who meant harm? Or perhaps an ally? He tried to get ready, but everything felt off, with all the variables his instincts screaming to retreat, but his body felt sluggish, uncoordinated. The stranger stopped and called over one shoulder. Caelen caught harsh consonants and rolling vowels, but not a single word he understood.

Caelen wanted to understand what was happening, but every attempt at comprehension failed under the weight of an unknown language and a body that refused to obey him perfectly. The stranger took another step closer, their presence filling his field of vision.
