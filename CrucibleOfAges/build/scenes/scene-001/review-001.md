# Scene Review

## Overall Assessment

The scene establishes Caelen's selfish persona and the inciting incident setup effectively. However, it contains a significant continuity error regarding the infant's status, which contradicts the established canon that the infant was already dead before the rescue. Additionally, the scene ends abruptly without depicting the actual rescue or death, failing to complete the primary objective of the first scene target.

## Findings

- [Canon] The scene implies the infant is alive or its status is ambiguous, contradicting the canon that the infant was already dead before the rescue.
  Evidence: In her arms, she held a bundle of cloth. Caelen’s trained eye picked out the details before his mind could process them. The bundle was still.
- [NarrativeDirection] The scene fails to depict Caelen's rescue death, which is the primary objective of the first scene target. It ends with the setup for the incident rather than the incident itself.
  Evidence: Caelen looked at the woman again. She had not moved. She was standing in the center of the road, her eyes closed, her body stiff with grief.

## Engagement Findings

- The scene ends on a cliffhanger without resolving the immediate dramatic question of Caelen's choice to shield the mother.

## Revision Guidance

- Clarify that the infant is dead, perhaps by describing the lack of movement or the mother's grief over a lifeless body.
- Extend the scene to include Caelen's decision to shield the mother and the subsequent gunshot wounds, completing the inciting incident.
