Caelen woke to the smell of wet stone and unwashed bodies. The cart’s suspension had groaned one last time before halting waking him up. He had gone in and out of sleep during the trip, but the trip couldn't have taken more than a few hours. They hadn't fed him nor given him any water. 

But it had been long enough for Mr. Halitosis to turn into Meeko. The two black eyes the guard that climbed into the cart was sporting made Caelen feel at least a little better about his own bruises. But it was with that thought that he realized that nothing really hurt. He was a bit sore, but nothing hurt like it did when he first woke up. Every bump in the road he had felt it. But he had eventually slept so either he had passed out or at some point the pain had gotten better.

Speaking in their throaty language Meeko formally known as Mr. Halitosis pulled Caelen to the edge of the cart and let him fall to the ground. Presumably deciding he didn't want to carry Caelen to wherever they were going he cut the binding on his feet. After beating the captive half to death, he likely figured there wasn't any fight left.  Which would explain the shock on his face as Meeko got his nose broken or at least returned to its formerly dislocation by the heel of a foot.  

When he awoke after the beating the next time Caelen found himself in a cell with thirty other captives, their forms where indistinct against the gloom. Some were wrapped in rags; others sat bare-chested, skin stained with soot or dried blood. A few eyes met his as he shifted his weight, testing the range of motion allowed.

The strangest part though was the positioning of the others in the cell. He was laying at the cell door. Caelen could only assume in the exact location he was dropped. As he uncurled his cramped muscles he hurt but not nearly enough. He had just been kicked unconscious twice in a short period, but nothing was broken, breathing only gave a slight twinge of pain and all the muscles so far had worked without cramping. He was in much better shape than he expected.
 
He tried to suppress the rising panic. Fear would get you beaten; indifference might keep you alive for another day. He focused on observation instead of reaction. On the other side of the bars near the cell door stood two guards in dull uniforms, spears resting casually against their shoulders. Their faces were hidden, shadowed by hoods. They moved without urgency, watching not him but someone deeper inside the pen.

On the far side, a man in purple sat at a rickety table seated on an overturned crate. Behind him stood a mamoth of a man with scars all over his upper body in a decretive pattern. These were lumps of scarred flesh that ran in spiralling arcs up his arms across his cheat up his nech and cheaks and across his forehead. They where each perfectly shaped while the ones on his face and hands where significantly smaller then those that ranged across his chest.

The scarred man’s posture betrayed exhaustion, yet he spoke with an air of authority. The purple figure said something I couldnt hear and likely couldnt understand is I did. He had flipped the page of a ledger, his black-tipped cane tapping rhythmically against the stone floor, as if conducting some unseen orchestra of humiliation.

Then came the strike.

A second guard from behind purples other shoulder stepped out and walked across the cell. As soon as he moved the room fell silent. That's when a girl crouching in the corner closest to him looked at him with such fear etched on her face that Caelen felt it. The Guard reached her and swung a cane hard across her shoulders. She cried out—a sound torn between pain and terror. Fresh blood seeped through the brown stains on her tunic as the guard pulled back his arm to strike again.

Her scream caught in Caelens chest like a stone dropped into water. Caelen had frozen for a heartbeat as he watched the first strike fall, but with that scream he forced himself to move. His muscles screamed. He had recovered more than he expected from his last beating but it all still hurt. Something deep inside required he act, it might have been the frightened eyes of a girl clutching a babe to her chest or it might be the fright in eyes of the girl before him but whatever it was, he moved.

In the silence he started crawling to his feet keeping his voice low but steady, he let out a loud "Hey! You want to get rough with someone? I'm all yours!" He was pretty happy they didn't understand him because the line was lame enough that he cringed. 

The guard scoffed, raising the cane again. Caelen kept talking as he finely reached his feet and started over. "Look at that. I’m just guessing here, right? You know how it is out there. Bad guys always pick the easiest targets first." Even though they didn't understand him they had stopped. 

He didn't really have a plan when he started this, but he was locked in now. They were only a few steps away. So naked bruised and barely able to walk in a new body with new gravity he stumbled the few steps and eventually fell over the girl covering her back with his.
