## Beats

#### Beat 1
Required Outcome: The void transitions from passive emptiness to active, intent-filled silence. A presence offers a binary choice between prioritizing the self or others, devoid of mechanical rules or rewards.

#### Beat 2
Required Outcome: Caelen rejects the path of self-interest, driven by revulsion for his past manipulations (Davis, his mother) and the sudden, clear peace he felt while shielding the mother. He chooses the path of sacrifice not as a tactic, but as a rejection of his former identity.

#### Beat 3
Required Outcome: The Creator manifests as an encompassing awareness. It verifies the choice and reveals that Earth is a source for candidates for ruling the universe. The Creator does not explain the mechanics of rule, the number of rulers, or the fate of those who fail; it only states that Caelen is a candidate and will return to live.
