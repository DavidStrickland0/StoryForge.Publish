## Beats

#### Beat 1

Required Outcome: Caelen perceives the absence of pain, gravity, and sensory input, realizing he is no longer in his physical body.

#### Beat 2

Required Outcome: He attempts to move or speak, but finds no limbs or voice, confirming the total loss of his physical form.

#### Beat 3

Required Outcome: He recalls the final moments of his death, specifically the peace he felt while shielding the mother, contrasting it with his previous selfish ambition.

#### Beat 4

Required Outcome: He experiences a moment of panic and disorientation, struggling to maintain his sense of self without the anchors of his Marine identity.


Purpose: Establish the sensory deprivation of the void and the immediate, disorienting aftermath of Caelen's death, forcing him to confront the absence of his physical body and military identity.

What changes: Caelen moves from the fading pain of his gunshot wounds to a state of suspended awareness in the void, where he realizes his body is gone and his previous status is irrelevant.
