## Beats

#### Beat 1

Required Outcome: Caelen perceives the passage of time in the void, which feels both instantaneous and endless, with no markers or events to anchor it.

#### Beat 2

Required Outcome: He attempts to recall specific details of his life on Earth, such as his mother's face or his squad's names, and finds them becoming blurry and difficult to grasp.

#### Beat 3

Required Outcome: The emotional intensity of his memories diminishes, leaving him with a sense of loss and detachment from his former self.

#### Beat 4

Required Outcome: He accepts his new state as a Sacrifice-aligned soul, ready for whatever comes next, even as his connection to his Earth life fades.


Purpose: Depict the subjective passage of time in the void and the gradual dilution of Caelen's memories, establishing the emotional and psychological cost of his wait.

What changes: Caelen experiences a long, indeterminate period in the void, during which his memories of Earth become less sharp and emotionally immediate, leaving him with a faded sense of his past life.
