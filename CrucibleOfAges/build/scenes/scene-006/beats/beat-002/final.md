Caelen was trying to get his head around the situation. He hadn't really thought much since he had arrived in this strange new world. He'd just been reacting. 

As he looked around the cell, the first thing he noticed was that there was some sort of meeting going on. Mr. Purple seemed to continue. He'd call out three words. Followed by a pause.

"Kael Varen    tha"
"Zane Korin    tha"
"Taren Voss    na"
After the third repetition, Scarface picked up a cane and approached an old man that immediately started begging. Scarface didn't pay any attention and just started beating on him. While that happened, Mr.  Purple continued.
"Rylan Zor    da" 
This time the man that had caned Caelen stepped over to another man and likewise started beating him. 

Caelen's stomach lurched watching the brutality. Something deep inside screamed at Caelen to move to help these men. Caelen had actually been surprised that he had moved so quickly to help the women beside him, barely awake he hadn't thought, he just followed instincts to protect her. The same instincts that were prompting him now to again move and protect but he had better control over it now. 
Mr. Purple continued 
"Kira Venn    tha"
"Dax Maren    tha"
"Vela Torin    cha"
Scarface left off his caning of the old man to approach a middle aged woman with an arm in a sling. Her face was hard and emotionless as he approached. As he got within reach she just crouched down and protected her arm with her body, presenting her back to the cane.
Again Caelen felt his instincts screaming at him to protect her. To stand between the women and the cane. It was odd, long ago he had felt the desire to protect an attractive women or two. Stepping in at a bar helping a bouncer etc. This was different though. The women wasn't really all that attractive and he even felt some push to protect the men that had gotten beaten. 
"Zarek Vale    tha"
"Nira Kalen    tha"
"Joren Tal     tha"

Everyone just stood and watched as the men beat the others. It was then Caelen realized that everyone wore a collar, both the beaten and the two slabs of muscle. The only person that didn't seem to have a collar on was the man in purple that seemed to be reading from table top.
There was another oddity. The room was crowded but no one but the girl and one other stood within ten cubits of him. He had first thought it was a by product of the beating. That they had given them space so they didn't get hit. But now people continued to crowd fairly close to the swinging cane, choosing to risk being hit rather then get any closer to them.
After another few lines and another beating was delivered, Mr. Purple stood and stepping around the table he walked toward the cell door with his two henchmen in tow. While the way to the door was clear, all three men seemed to walk around the group of three staying that same ten cubits away only walking through the outer edge of the open space. They gave no reaction or indication why they stayed away they just did.
Finally reaching the door they knocked on it. The guards unlocked the door and let them out.
