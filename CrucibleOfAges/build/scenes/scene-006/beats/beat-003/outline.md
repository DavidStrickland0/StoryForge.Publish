**Purpose:** Caelen confronts the systemic brutality of the captors' hierarchy, testing his instinct to protect while navigating his role in this oppressive structure.  

**Viewpoint:** Caelen’s internal conflict between his instinct to intervene and his growing awareness of the system’s cruelty.  
**Objective:** To observe and react to the captors’ ritualized violence, while assessing whether he can act without losing himself to the cycle of exploitation.  
**Meaningful Change:** Caelen begins to question whether protecting the girl is an act of sacrifice or another step toward self-preservation.  
**Continuity Dependencies:** The ongoing violence, the presence of the girl, and the unspoken rules of the captors’ hierarchy must remain unresolved, pushing Caelen toward a choice that could redefine his path.
