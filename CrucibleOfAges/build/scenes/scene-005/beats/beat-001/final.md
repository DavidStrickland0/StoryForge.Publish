The scarred man moved with a slow, grinding gait that seemed to drag the air around him. He did not look at the guard who had paused; his eyes were fixed on Caelen as if he could smell the unfamiliar tension in the captive's frame. Scarface stopped near the girl, looming over her without touching her, and extended a hand toward Caelen.

Caelen kept his gaze on the ground between them, refusing to meet those eyes. He understood none of the harsh consonants that rolled into the cell like stones skipping across water, but he saw the man’s posture shift from aggression to something resembling assessment. The guard with the cane remained at attention, a statue of dull obedience, while Scarface leaned in close enough for Caelen to feel the heat radiating off him—a warmth that felt wrong to close to familiar.

The man spoke again. His voice was low, dripping with a rhythm Caelen couldn't parse, yet the cadence suggested authority rather than command with those universal tones you use to sooth a pet or a crying baby. Caelen relaxed at the sound, his muscles screaming in protest from the fresh cut on his back. He wanted to shrink away, to curl into the smallest knot of flesh possible, but the girl’s grip on his arm held him steady. Her nails digging shallow crescents into his skin, an anchor he didn't fight against.

Caelen swallowed hard, the motion painful in a throat that felt too tight. He was naked, exposed, and sore, yet there was a strange clarity in his mind as he watched these figures who ruled this place. They were not monsters; they were simply the gears of a machine he did not understand his actions. Scarface reached out a hand again, palm up. It was an invitation, or perhaps a command to stand, but Caelen didn't move. He stayed low, one knee pressing into the damp earth, keeping his body positioned between the girl and any potential violence.

The silence stretched, filled only by the distant rumble of the cart outside and the ragged breathing of thirty captives huddled in the gloom. Scarface waited, his expression unreadable behind a mask of soot and old scars. Caelen held his breath, waiting to see if the invitation would turn into a blow or something else entirely. He had no plan, only the instinct to protect the small, trembling life beneath him.

With a gesture and a command from Scarface, The Cane moved back to his position behind purple.
