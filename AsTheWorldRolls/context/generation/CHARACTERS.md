# As the World Rolls — Characters

The immutable Chapters 1–14 remain the authority for each character's earlier history. The state below is the endpoint of the rebuilt Chapters 15–18, not a revision of those earlier chapters. Character identifiers are retained.

## Elias Thorne

Elias is recovering at the Gilded Tankard outside the watchfort with Kaelen, Maren, Torin, and Elara. His existing side wound reopened during exertion and received ordinary cleaning and a fresh bandage at the fort. His leather armor, sword, dagger, and bronze map remain his property. He reclaimed the map after Elara and Kaelen checked the copies for the observation post. He knows the local feed closed and independent watches corroborated that result; he does not know that every danger in the ruined city is gone. He has accepted help and transferred the watch instead of keeping the group in the valley. No caravan contract has been signed.

## Kaelen

Kaelen is a young mage with an established connection to the Blight and the brass gauntlet. The stronger pull in 15 exhausted him. He remained himself, described his limits, and helped by listening rather than attempting another stronger pull. The channel holds after he moves outside range. He received care and slept at the watchfort. At the endpoint he carries the gauntlet unworn in his folded coat and is not secretly powering the closure. Earlier injuries and abilities remain as established in the immutable text. The forest keeper and the gauntlet's full nature remain uncertain; no new power, immunity, origin, or mandatory sacrifice is established.

## Maren

Maren's hands were burned while she and Torin used her hammer haft to restrict the groove. The temporary hold was explicitly replaced before she rested or left. Her palms were cleaned and bandaged at the watchfort. In 18 she paid the smith to replace the damaged haft on her existing hammer and kept the old blackened wood as a reminder. She retains her practical, direct voice and established non-romantic companionship. She has not bought caustic sulfur paste or discovered a universal Blight repellent in the rebuilt continuation.

## Torin

Torin is the established soldier and companion. He caught Kaelen when the quartz failed, shared the temporary hammer restriction with Maren, kept the ditch creature back with his sword, carried the map on the return journey, and helped document the safer route and supplies needing replacement. He received rest at the watchfort rather than joining a new expedition immediately. His sword and familiar equipment remain. No unperformed caravan or raider-camp campaign is part of his history.

## Elara

Elara is the scholar who operated the chisel and quartz in Chapter 14. In 15 she sustained a cut on the hand, tested the broken fragments, and fixed their working angles using stone wedges and a leather sling. In 16 she corrected a displaced support and closed the feed incrementally. She retains her chisel, loose quartz dust, notes, and diagrams; the three working fragments stay in the closed shrine groove. Her usable account is given to the watchfort. She does not possess a conveniently prewritten solution to the new failure, prove a universal mosaic magic system, or establish a new lunar-cycle/siphon theory in this continuation.

## Other established figures

The stranger, the forest keeper, and all other figures and relationships established before or during Chapter 14 remain as written. Do not merge identities, replace names, invent their deaths, or erase those earlier appearances. Unnamed watchfort officers, guards, attendants, a clerk, the smith, and inn staff carry the ordinary work concretely introduced in the rebuilt aftermath. They do not become secret antagonists or new quest-givers.
