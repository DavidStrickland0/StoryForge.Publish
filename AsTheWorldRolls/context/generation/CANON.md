# StoryForge Endless — CANON

## Established Canon


- A village characterized by black spires exists.


- The city has been under the shadow of the Blight for three weeks.


- The Blight is a corruption spreading through the world that creates a living fog and corrupts creatures into Blight-Touched entities.


- Blight-Touched creatures are humanoid, too tall, too pale, with skin stretched taut over protruding bones, glowing sickly green eyes, and jerky unnatural gaits.


- Blight-Touched creatures are highly resistant to physical damage but vulnerable to specific magical weaknesses and disruption.


- The fog associated with the Blight reduces visibility to nearly zero within ten paces.


- Sudden noises or quick movements in the Blight fog trigger attacks from lurking entities.


- Kaelen's magic is fueled by the same energy that fuels the Blight.


- Blight-Touched entities are capable of visible self-repair and regeneration of corrupted flesh.


- The temple was built to contain the Blight.


- The temple contains a lower passage beneath the altar leading to a network of channels.


- The temple's barrier functions as a trap that draws corruption in and consumes it rather than just containing it.


- The temple was built around springs where water used in binding rites entered clean and left without crossing the same ground.


- A bronze plate map exists showing the temple, six smaller shrines, and a web of channels beneath the city.


- The map indicates a route west beyond the outer wall ending at a symbol resembling a split crown.


- The map shows a way station or second refuge near the western edge of the city.


- A band of pale light exists in the lower passage connecting to the glowing runes around the temple entrance.


- The Blight can turn the dead into weapons that can be commanded by whatever is outside.


- Kaelen's magic can hurt the Blight.


- The forest contains a sentient presence or memory that remembers past events and entities buried within it.


- The watchfort exists at the edge of the forest, built into a hillside with thick dark walls and narrow slit-like windows.


- A single lantern burns in the main gate of the watchfort.


- The path from the forest to the watchfort is clear and leads down a gentle slope.


- Maren exists and is an established character.


- Elara exists and is an established character.


- The gatehouse interior smells of old wood, drying herbs, and rusted iron.


- The watchfort walls are thick, pitted by wind and rain, and have slit-like windows.

## Changes Over Time


- The barrier previously flickered and weakened; it now stands stronger after absorbing the Blight.
