## Revision Guidance

- Correct the location description to match the stone shrine setting.
- Remove references to the gauntlet re-igniting; keep it dark and cold.
- Advance the plot by having the group attempt a new method or move toward the next milestone instead of resting indefinitely.
- Condense the repeated technical explanations about shard arrangement into a single, concise moment.
- Clarify the barrier's thermal properties: either establish that it emits heat as a byproduct of consumption or remove references to burning hands.
- Reconcile the gauntlet's temperature: if Kaelen is channeling energy, describe the gauntlet as warm or vibrating; if it is cold, explain why the energy does not transfer heat to the metal.
- Establish resource scarcity: explicitly mention the lack of food, water, or currency to maintain the tone of survival and hardship.
- Clarify the barrier's color changes: link them to specific events (e.g., absorption of Blight) rather than arbitrary shifts.
- Address the group's exhaustion: describe the physical toll of resting for hours after intense exertion, such as trembling limbs or dizziness.
- Reconcile the barrier's stability: explain how the shattered shards can maintain resonance without disrupting the frequency.
- Clarify the barrier's function: ensure consistency between 'consumption' and 'blockage' mechanics.
