"It holds," she said, her voice barely rising above the drone. "The frequency is consistent. We aren't fighting the flow anymore; we are riding it."

Torin’s eyes followed the movement, ensuring Elias kept his distance from the connection point, but he made no move to intervene. The hierarchy of the moment was clear: Elias assessed the strategic picture, Maren and Torin provided physical containment and safety, Elara managed the mechanism, and Kaelen served as the conduit.

"Kaelen," Elias said, his voice low and steady. "Can you hear the change?"
