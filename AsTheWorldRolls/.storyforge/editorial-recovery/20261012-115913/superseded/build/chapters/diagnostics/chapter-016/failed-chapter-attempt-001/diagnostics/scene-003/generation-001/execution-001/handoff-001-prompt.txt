He kept his gaze forward, watching Maren and Torin as they moved toward the tree line to verify the perimeter one last time. "Clear," Maren called out after a long pause, her voice carrying easily over the wind that had finally picked up again. "Nothing stirring in the ditches. The fog is gone entirely."

Torin nodded from the edge of the brush, his hand resting casually on his sword hilt though there was no need to draw it. "The barrier's scar is knitting shut," he added, glancing back at Elias. "It looks like a bruise that's already fading. No more hum. No more pull."

Elias slowed his pace, allowing the group to catch up as they stepped over the final ridge of the shrine platform and onto the main valley floor. The stone here was no longer warm; it had cooled quickly once the resonance ceased, leaving behind a smooth, dark surface that reflected the first stars appearing in the deepening sky.

Kaelen walked beside him, his arms hanging loose at his sides. The brass gauntlet on his left wrist sat cold and inert against his skin, its runes dimmed to a dull gray. He hadn't spoken since they'd begun moving away from the fissure, but his posture was relaxed, the tension that had kept his shoulders hunched for the last hour finally melting away.
