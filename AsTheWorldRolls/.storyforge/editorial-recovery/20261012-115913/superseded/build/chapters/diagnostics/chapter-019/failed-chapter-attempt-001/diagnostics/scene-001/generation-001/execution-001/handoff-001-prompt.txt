The air smelled of wet wool, horse manure, and the sharp tang of sulfur paste that Maren had insisted they carry.

Torin stood at the head of the formation, his hands on the reins of the lead draft team. "And the crate?" Maren asked, not looking up from her inspection of the rigging.

"The sealed container is mounted to the lead wagon," Torin confirmed. "Double-latched. Elias and I will stay at the front. If anyone comes out of nowhere, we’re the first to see it."
