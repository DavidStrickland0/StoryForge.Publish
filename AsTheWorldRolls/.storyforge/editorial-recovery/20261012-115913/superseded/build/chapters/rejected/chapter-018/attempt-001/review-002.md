# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter suffers from a severe Continuity defect by placing the party in the Gilded Tankard tavern before they have left the shrine location established at the end of Chapter 16. It also repeats the 'mosaic frequency' breakthrough as an active, ongoing operation rather than a completed development, and introduces new threats (creature near gates) without establishing how they survived the immediate aftermath of the barrier stabilization.

## Revision Guidance

- End Chapter 16 with the party stabilizing the barrier and waiting for Watchfort confirmation. Do not transition them to the tavern until that confirmation arrives or they decide to leave.
- Treat the 'mosaic frequency' breakthrough as a completed development from Chapter 16. Do not describe it as an ongoing, fragile operation requiring active tuning in Chapter 17.
- Resolve the Blight prelude arc by consuming or containing the local source before accepting new contracts for the Frontier Road.
- Remove the 'creature near gates' threat until after the Blight prelude is concluded and the party has left the city.
- Advance the narrative by having the party leave the shrine location, not by repeating the same scene at a different location.
- Do not manufacture new threats (creature, deadline) solely to create artificial momentum after the immediate danger has been resolved.
- Establish the ResolutionCriteria for the Blight prelude arc (local source destroyed/contained) before moving on to the next contract.
- Do not contradict the hierarchy between Short, Medium, and Long arcs by prematurely ending the Blight prelude without resolving its objectives.
- Do not use activity of any kind (negotiation, route planning) without changing character, relationship, knowledge, choices, circumstances, consequences, or the route forward regarding the Blight.
- Do not introduce a new attack, arrival, revelation, sound, figure, pursuit, deadline, or imminent threat merely to make the final paragraphs feel dramatic.
- Do not turn the completion of an immediate objective (stabilizing barrier) into an automatic discovery of a larger version of the same danger.
- Do not resolve an arc without establishing its ResolutionCriteria.
- Clarify the financial state: Either establish that the initial 'small pile' was a deposit or partial payment that is being deducted from the final 'upfront' amount, or remove the mention of the coins appearing if they were not actually received. Ensure the arithmetic of the transaction (initial consultation fee vs. final contract payment) is clear.
- If the initial coins were a gift or separate tip, explicitly state that they are distinct from the contractual payment to avoid confusion about available cash flow.
