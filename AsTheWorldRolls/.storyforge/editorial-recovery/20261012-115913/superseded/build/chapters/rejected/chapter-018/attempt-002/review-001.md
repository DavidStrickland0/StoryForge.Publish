# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains significant continuity errors regarding character presence (Torin's location), financial logic (unexplained coin pouch), and narrative progression (repeating completed developments). The prose also exhibits a sustained pattern of explaining character motives that are already clear from action, violating style rules.

## Findings

- [Canon] Torin is established as being inside the Gilded Tankard tavern in Chapter 17's finalized ending. This chapter opens with him 'already moving' and leaving the tavern, then later describes him buying supplies at a market stall outside. The previous chapter explicitly states he was preparing to head outside but does not confirm departure; this chapter treats his departure as a completed fact without establishing the transition.
  Evidence: Torin was already moving before the last echo of their agreement had faded into the tavern's ambient roar. He didn't announce his departure; he simply stood, his joints cracking softly, and walked toward the door.
- [FinancialLogic] Elias withdraws a 'small pouch of coins' to pay for the map separately from the contract deposit. The chapter provides no prior establishment of this specific sum or its source. Financial rules require tracking implied cash flow and separating received money from promised wages; spending an unexplained pouch contradicts the requirement to keep dollars consistent and avoid vague deprivation.
  Evidence: He reached into his coat and withdrew a small pouch of coins. He didn't place them on the table; he simply held them up, letting the metal clink softly in the silence that had fallen around their booth.

## Revision Guidance

- Establish Torin's departure in Chapter 16 or confirm his presence in Chapter 17 before having him leave.
- Remove the unexplained pouch of coins; use established funds or describe acquiring them earlier.
- Delete redundant explanations of Maren's and Elias's motives after their actions are clear.
- Correct Maren's gauntlet description to match Canon.md (she has a hammer/mace).
- Ensure Kaelen's gauntlet state matches Chapter 17's ending (dark/inert) unless explicitly changed.
- Avoid clipped sentence patterns ('It was good. It was necessary.') throughout the chapter.
- Establish a clear coin count for Elias before he offers the pouch for the map. Show him counting out coins or acknowledging his limited reserve.
- Show Torin checking his purse or drawing from a shared fund when purchasing supplies. Indicate the weight of the coin purse decreasing or the specific amount spent.
- Clarify that the 'fifty percent upfront' is a promise for future delivery, not immediate cash in hand, so spending on supplies before payment is received is logical.
- Show Maren performing the repair with her existing tools immediately after buying the strips to maintain physical coherence.
- Limit Torin's purchases to what fits in his pack or show him making multiple trips if he buys large quantities like undergarments for everyone.
