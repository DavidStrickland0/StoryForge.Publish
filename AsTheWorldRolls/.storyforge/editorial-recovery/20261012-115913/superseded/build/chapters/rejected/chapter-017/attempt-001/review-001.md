# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains a significant Continuity defect regarding the party's location and state. It incorrectly places the group inside the Gilded Tankard tavern after they have just stabilized the barrier at the Gate of Silence shrine in Chapter 16. The narrative fails to account for the travel time required to reach the watchfort or a tavern, contradicting the established canon that the party is resting at the shrine awaiting Watchfort confirmation. Additionally, the chapter repeats the 'mosaic frequency' breakthrough as an active event rather than treating it as a completed development from Chapter 16.

## Findings

- [Continuity] The chapter places the party inside the Gilded Tankard tavern immediately after the events of Chapter 16, where they were resting at the Gate of Silence shrine. This contradicts the established canon that the party is waiting for confirmation from the Watchfort before moving on. The narrative fails to account for the travel required to reach a tavern or the watchfort.
  Evidence: The heavy oak door of the Gilded Tankard groaned in protest... Torin pushed the door open, his hand braced against the wood to steady it against the wind that still drifted through the valley pass. The party filed in...
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the 'mosaic frequency' breakthrough as an active event rather than treating it as a completed development from Chapter 16. It describes Elara arranging shards and Kaelen guiding the flow, which contradicts the canon that this was already achieved in Chapter 16.
  Evidence: "The resonance is stable now. The barrier is feeding the Blight at a controlled rate..." followed by later dialogue explaining the mechanics of the mosaic frequency as if it were new information.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter ignores the active Short arc's NextMilestone (waiting for Watchfort confirmation) to pursue unrelated tavern socializing. It resolves the immediate threat of the Blight source prematurely without establishing that the city foundations are ready, contradicting the hierarchy between arcs.
  Evidence: "We're not going to lose anyone... That's a promise we made to each other." followed by discussions with Silas Vane about contracts and supply lines, bypassing the unresolved situation of the Blight containment.
- [Canon] The chapter contradicts established canon regarding the party's location. Canon states the group is resting at the Gate of Silence shrine awaiting confirmation from the Watchfort. The chapter depicts them entering a tavern in the valley, implying they have left the shrine without justification.
  Evidence: "Torin pushed the door open... The party filed in..." This action implies movement away from the shrine location established in Chapter 16 without narrative support.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter resolves the local Blight counterattack prematurely by having the party rest and negotiate contracts, contradicting the ResolutionCriteria for the medium-blight-prelude arc which requires the source to be destroyed or sealed before moving on.
  Evidence: "For now, they were safe. They had food, shelter, and companionship." This statement implies the immediate threat is fully resolved, contradicting the canon that the local source must be contained before leaving.
- [FinancialLogic] The party pays a lodging deposit of 'three nights' stay plus an additional sum for 'extraordinary circumstances', yet the narrative immediately establishes they are resting for only one night before moving on, and no mechanism is provided to cover the cost of the remaining two nights or the extraordinary fee.
  Evidence: The keeper states: 'the contract requires a deposit of gold coins equal to three nights' stay plus an additional sum for what we call 'extraordinary circumstances.' Elara counts them carefully before placing them on the table. 'This is enough for three nights plus an extra sum,' she said.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter repeats the 'mosaic frequency' breakthrough as an active event rather than treating it as a completed development from Chapter 16.

## Revision Guidance

- Move the scene to the watchfort or have the party travel there before entering a tavern. Treat the mosaic frequency as a known fact established in Chapter 16.
- Ensure the narrative waits for confirmation from the Watchfort before allowing the party to leave the shrine area.
- Clarify whether the deposit covers only the current night or if the party has a plan to pay for subsequent nights (e.g., they will leave early, or the 'extraordinary circumstances' fee is a one-time security deposit that does not require nightly payment).
- If the contract requires three nights of payment but the party intends to stay only one night, establish a reason why the tavern accepts this arrangement (e.g., the party leaves before the third night, or the fee is non-refundable and covers potential risks).
