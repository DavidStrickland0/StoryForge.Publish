# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter fails FinancialLogic by conflateing restricted lodging deposits with available cash for supplies. It also violates NarrativeDirection by repeating the 'rest' state established in Chapter 16 without advancing the Blight containment or moving toward the Dragon Road.

## Findings

- [FinancialLogic] The chapter treats a lodging deposit of three nights plus an extraordinary sum as if it can be paid from current cash, while simultaneously claiming the party has no supplies and needs water skins. This conflates restricted future payments with immediate purchasing power.
  Evidence: "Elara stepped forward... produced a small pouch of coins. She counted them carefully before placing them on the table. 'This is enough for three nights plus an extra sum,' she said."
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the 'rest' and 'waiting' state from Chapter 16 without producing meaningful change or advancing the Blight containment arc. The party waits for Vane's riders instead of moving toward the Dragon Road objective.
  Evidence: "There was still work to do before dawn, but for now, they could rest in peace. The road ahead would be long and difficult, but they were stronger together than apart."
- [FinancialLogic] The tavern keeper demands a deposit equal to 'three nights' stay plus an additional sum for extraordinary circumstances,' but Elara pays only a small pouch of coins without establishing that the amount covers the full demand or specifying the value of the coins.
  Evidence: "Elara stepped forward, her expression serious as she reached into her satchel and produced a small pouch of coins. She counted them carefully before placing them on the table. 'This is enough for three nights plus an extra sum,' she said. The keeper nodded, taking the coins without looking at them. 'That's exactly what we need.'"
- [FinancialLogic] The party states they are heading east toward the Dragon Road, but the tavern is located in the valley pass near the shrine fissure where they just stabilized the barrier, creating a geographical inconsistency that undermines the plausibility of securing immediate multi-night lodging before moving on.
  Evidence: "'We're not from these parts then?' 'No,' Elias admitted. 'We're heading eastward, toward the Dragon Road.' ... The keeper nodded slowly, his eyes drifting back to the fire. 'Dragon Road... that'll be a long haul if you're going all the way there.'"

## Engagement Findings

None.

## Revision Guidance

- Clarify that the lodging deposit is a future obligation or credit, not current cash.
- Advance the plot by having the party depart for the Dragon Road or face a new obstacle.
- Clarify the value of the coins Elara produces to ensure they cover the full deposit demand, or have the keeper explicitly confirm acceptance of the partial payment.
- Resolve the geographical inconsistency by either confirming the tavern is indeed in the valley pass near the shrine (consistent with Chapter 16) and adjusting the party's stated destination, or relocating the tavern to a more logical stopping point on the Dragon Road.
