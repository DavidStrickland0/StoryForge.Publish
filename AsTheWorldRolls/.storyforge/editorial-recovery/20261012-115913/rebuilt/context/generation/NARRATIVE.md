# As the World Rolls — Narrative

## Authority and episode boundary

Chapters 1–14, ending with **Chapter 14: The Gate of Silence**, are immutable canon. The rebuilt Chapters 15–18 replace the old continuation. This episode ends at Chapter 18. Unused structural arc slots are series reserves, not a requirement to generate Chapter 19.

## Immutable handoff at the end of Chapter 14

Elias, Kaelen, Maren, Torin, and Elara are at the Gate of Silence shrine. The consuming barrier is at the temple across the valley. The quartz-and-chisel note has opened a controlled feed, the first phase is complete, and the stronger second phase has just begun. Kaelen must pull; the increased load has not yet succeeded or failed in the immutable chapter. No completed local drainage, departure, return, or new contract may be inserted into that history.

## Rebuilt chronology

- **15 — The Broken Valve:** Increased flow fractures the quartz. Elara is cut, Kaelen is exhausted, and Maren and Torin temporarily restrict the groove with the hammer haft. A tested three-fragment arrangement replaces the temporary manual restriction. The party recovers limited control; the reservoir is still feeding the temple. Nothing is prematurely declared resolved.
- **16 — The Last Note:** A creature in the ditch draws off the feed. Adjusting a displaced fragment redirects the corruption to the distant consuming barrier. The local reservoir pressure ceases. The party withdraws the note, narrows and closes the groove, waits for a surge, then tests the closure from outside range. The mechanism holds without Kaelen or anyone else maintaining it. All five leave together.
- **17 — The Road Back:** The return journey is shown. The injured party arrives at the watchfort, receives treatment, and supplies a usable, candid report. Independent observers take over the next watch. Equipment is accounted for; Kaelen removes the gauntlet. No one abandons an active operation off-page.
- **18 — A Place at the Table:** Independent reports corroborate the absent renewed feed and steady temple barrier. The city remains restricted until it can be inspected. The party finishes its account, checks map copies, replaces Maren's damaged hammer haft, and takes food and rooms at the Gilded Tankard outside the watchfort. All five have a stable stopping point. There is no merchant contract, caravan departure, summons, or replacement threat.

## Current state at the ending

The local Blight operation is finished. The closed groove retains the three working shards. The watchfort has the account, route copies, placement drawings, and observation responsibility. The companions are recovering at the Gilded Tankard. Elias's wound remains bandaged, Maren's palms need care, and Elara's cut is healing; none are magically restored overnight. Kaelen can remain disconnected from the channel and leave his gauntlet unworn.

## Deliberately bounded uncertainty

The party does not know whether every Blight manifestation elsewhere is gone, whether the enormous silhouette in the ruins is dead, or the complete nature of the forest keeper. The ending does not invent those answers. Local victory is verified and responsibility transferred; uncertainty is neither an immediate peril nor a cliffhanger compelling another chapter.
