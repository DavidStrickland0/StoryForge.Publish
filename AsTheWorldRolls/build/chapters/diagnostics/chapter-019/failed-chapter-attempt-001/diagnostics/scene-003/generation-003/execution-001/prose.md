Maren did not say anything about her own state; she simply nodded and turned toward the crate near the hearth where supplies lay scattered—a small water skin half full, a roll of bandages, and Elias's bronze map case. Kaelen closed his eyes as the last wave of magic drained from his chest, leaving him hollowed out but warm. "Do not speak until you have food," Torin said. "We need him coherent."

Torin stepped fully inside the room and closed the doorway against the wind, his scarred face a mask of grim determination. The group's unity was solidified by this shared decision to continue west with him rather than abandon him or wait for dawn.

"He is breathing," Elias replied. "And his resonance has shifted to push rather than pull. The flow is controlled."

Elara remained seated on the floor with her notebook closed, watching Kaelen rise. She did not speak, but her sharp eyes noted every detail: the way his hands shook slightly from exhaustion, the lack of fear in his movements, and the deliberate care he took to avoid overexerting himself.

The channel entrance at the Gate of Silence was exposed, connected to the shrine via the channel network.
