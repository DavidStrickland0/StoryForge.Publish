## Revision Guidance

- Delay the departure decision until after a proper assessment of Kaelen's physical state and the group's consensus on moving vs. staying.
- Advance the narrative from the 'fragile peace' state by introducing new complications or discoveries rather than repeating it.
- Earn any escalation (departure) through genuine plot developments, not artificial momentum.
- Establish ResolutionCriteria for Kaelen's waking before concluding the arc.
- Focus on the primary narrative action: assessing Kaelen's readiness and deciding whether to move or stay.
- Establish the physical properties of Kaelen's gauntlet and the Blight energy before he attempts to warm Maren's hands.
- Describe the mechanism of Elias's heat extraction system more concretely before he adjusts the grate.
- Clarify Maren's role as a radiator and her physical limitations regarding thermal load.
- Show the group packing supplies and preparing for departure before moving toward the door.
- Demonstrate how Elara uses the quartz stone to detect resonance frequency, perhaps by tapping it against a surface or placing it in a fissure.
- Explain why Torin places his hand on Kaelen's shoulder and what physical support he is providing.
- Describe the condition of the drainage channels before Elara suggests walking through them.
- Show Elias checking the dagger for nicks only if there is a reason to do so, such as preparing for combat or cutting something.
