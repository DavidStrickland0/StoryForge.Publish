- The chapter repeats the 'deciphering ancient runes' beat from Chapter 5 without advancing the mystery of what the temple contains or how it relates to the Blight's hive mind.
- The chapter fails to escalate the tension established in Chapter 5 regarding the massive structure emerging, instead opting for a quiet exploration sequence.

- Acknowledge the active threat of the massive entity pressing against the barrier immediately upon entering the scene.
- Clarify the spatial layout: ensure the 'large chamber' is consistent with the temple's description (ruins, narrow passages) and does not contradict the fog conditions.
- Reintegrate Kaelen's brass gauntlet into the narrative or explain its absence if it was lost/damaged.
- Ensure the transition from Chapter 5's ending (huddled together after erecting barrier) is logical and doesn't reset their location.
- Address the immediate danger of the approaching entity rather than treating the situation as a calm exploration.
- Avoid describing the ceiling as 'open to the sky' if the temple is in a city under Blight fog where visibility is nearly zero.
- Remove the reference to the ruined fountain from Kaelen's perspective inside the temple.
- Clarify that Elias is recalling the sword as a relic of a past era rather than describing it as currently possessed.
- Establish a clear magical or environmental cause for the 'pulse' and 'shifting' carvings before attributing these effects to them.
- Ensure descriptions of stone objects remain consistent with established physical laws unless explicitly overridden by magic.
