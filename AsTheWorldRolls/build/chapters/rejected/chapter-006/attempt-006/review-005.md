- The chapter fails to escalate the immediate threat of the massive entity approaching outside the barrier, instead focusing on internal exploration which reduces tension.
- The dialogue repeats themes of 'understanding' and 'deciphering' without advancing the plot or resolving the conflict.

- Integrate the immediate threat of the massive entity approaching outside rather than treating it as a distant abstract concept.
