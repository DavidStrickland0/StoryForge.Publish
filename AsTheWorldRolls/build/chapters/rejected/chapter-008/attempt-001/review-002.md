## Engagement Findings

- The chapter fails to provide new narrative tension or progression by recycling the climax of the previous chapter's encounter.
- The repetition of dialogue and action renders the scene static and unengaging for a reader who has already experienced the event in Chapter 7.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the entire sequence describing the entity's appearance, speech, and dissolution. This event was fully resolved in Chapter 7.
- Replace the repeated dialogue with new interactions that explore the immediate aftermath of the encounter or advance the journey toward the watchfort.
- Introduce a new complication or sensory detail related to the forest's 'memory' that has not yet been explored, rather than re-explaining what the entity already said.
- Ensure the chapter moves forward from the state established at the end of Chapter 7 (post-encounter) rather than re-enacting the encounter itself.
- Remove the duplicated narrative sequence starting from 'The forest pressed in around them...' through to the end of the chapter.
- Ensure each action (kneeling, speaking, reacting) occurs only once per encounter unless explicitly separated by time or a new stimulus.
