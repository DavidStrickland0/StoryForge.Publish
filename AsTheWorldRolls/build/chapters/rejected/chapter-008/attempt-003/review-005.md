- The chapter repeats the discovery of the forest's memory without advancing the plot or resolving the current question of how to reach the watchfort.
- The chapter ends with a cliffhanger about something waiting for them, but does not provide any new information or action to advance the narrative.

- Remove references to returning to the city or party, as they are already traveling west toward a watchfort.
- Replace the abstract philosophical descriptions of Kaelen with actions consistent with his established characterization.
- Advance the plot by having them encounter a specific challenge or obstacle related to the forest's memory rather than simply walking deeper into it.
- Provide new information or action at the end of the chapter to advance the narrative.
- Ensure Elias does not draw a sword if he has no blade; clarify whether he possesses one or if the 'dull edge' is metaphorical.
- Provide a physical cause for Kaelen's lantern flickering (e.g., wind, magical interference) or remove the description if unintended.

- Align Elias's internal monologue about having no blade with his external action of drawing a sword.
- Ensure the decision to stop searching is consistent with their stated goal of finding the memory.
- Verify that Elias's understanding of the temple's containment capabilities matches the established canon.
- Add a physical trigger for Kaelen's gauntlet tightening (e.g., magical resonance, cold touch).
- Maintain consistency between the characters' knowledge and their actions throughout the scene.
