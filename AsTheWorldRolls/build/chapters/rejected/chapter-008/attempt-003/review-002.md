# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains significant continuity errors regarding character equipment (specifically Elias's sword), misrepresents established mechanical properties of the Blight-Touched, and fails to advance the narrative by re-performing actions that were already completed in Chapter 7.

However, Canon.md establishes: 'Elias Thorne possesses a sword that is dull from disuse.' Additionally, later in the chapter, Kaelen says: 'Kaelen nodded, his hand tightening around his sword,' implying Elias also has one or the narration is confused about who holds what.

While these are traits of Blight-Touched, the context implies this is the 'keeper' or ancient presence they just identified as distinct from the Blight. The description conflates the two entities in a way that contradicts the established distinction that the 'keeper' is an ancient memory/presence, not a Blight-Touched creature.

It did not move like a creature of flesh and bone.' Immediately after, Kaelen says: 'That's not—' he started... It was humanoid, but not quite.' The text then explicitly states: 'This was not the Blight. This was something else.' However, the visual description (pale skin, glowing eyes) matches the Blight-Touched definition in Canon.md ('glowing sickly green eyes', 'too pale'), creating a contradiction where the 'keeper' looks exactly like the enemy they just distinguished themselves from.
