## NonBlocking

This description is too close to Blight-Touched ('too tall... skin stretched taut over protruding bones') and creates confusion about whether this is a new variant or if the distinction between the Keeper and Blight-Touched is being blurred inappropriately.

Validation: The finding claims the description creates confusion and blurs the distinction between the Keeper and Blight-Touched. However, the chapter explicitly states, 'This was not the Blight. This was something else,' directly addressing the potential for reader confusion. While the physical description is vague and shares traits with Blight-Touched (elongation), the narrative intent is clearly to introduce a new, distinct entity ('the keeper') that operates on a different plane ('memory', 'presence').
