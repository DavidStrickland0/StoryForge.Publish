# Chapter Review

## Revision Guidance

- Advance the narrative by having the group depart Pick and Axe for the western way station.
- Depict Kaelen as requiring support due to his exhaustion, not operating independently.
- Maintain Maren's lack of certification and tremors during critical operations.
- Ensure the quartz stone remains cold and inert until reactivated by specific mechanics.
- Address the entity in the spires as a continuing threat rather than resolving it.
- Clarify Maren's tremor: either establish it as a chronic condition that requires management rather than a habit she developed to hide, or explain why her hands stopped shaking after the test.
- Explain the source of heat and vibration in the quartz stone: ensure there is a consistent mechanism for how the stone generates energy (e.g., Kaelen's resonance, ambient magic, or stored charge).
- Address the activation of Kaelen's gauntlet: provide a logical trigger for why the dormant relic activates when he touches the stone.
- Reconsider the group's decision to rest: ensure they address the immediate threat (unstable barrier, active Blight) before deciding to eat and sleep.
- Maintain consistency in character descriptions: avoid contradicting earlier assessments of a character's physical capabilities (e.g., Maren's precision vs. her tremor).
- Ensure medical interventions are consistent: if Maren's condition improves, show the necessary steps (salve, rest, monitoring) rather than having it resolve magically.
- Clarify the timeline: ensure the group's actions align with the established urgency of the situation and the known mechanics of the Blight.
