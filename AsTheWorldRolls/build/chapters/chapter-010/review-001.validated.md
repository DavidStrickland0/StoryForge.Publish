## Blocking

Category: PhysicalLogic

The brass gauntlet is described as inert and cold after stopping pulsing, yet later emits a faint rhythmic pulse of light again while Kaelen is sitting still, violating the established physical state of the object.

Validation: The finding correctly identifies a direct contradiction in the physical state of the brass gauntlet. The chapter first establishes that the gauntlet has stopped pulsing and is 'cold and inert.' Later, in the same continuous sequence of events (the reunion and subsequent discussion in the gatehouse), the text describes the gauntlet as emitting a 'faint, rhythmic pulse of light' and having a 'subtle glow.'
