The second time, he had felt afraid. The third time, he had felt something colder, sharper.

Leverage.

His expression was that of a man conducting a routine audit, though his eyes betrayed a flicker of underlying tension.

"I’m feeling fine, Mr. Miller," Adrian said, his voice steady. "But I have some questions about the compensation."

It will be deposited directly into your account within the hour. In exchange, you will remain in the hospital for the duration of the stabilization period. You will not leave. You will not speak to anyone outside the medical team about your condition or the data."

"Withdrawal of consent?" Adrian repeated, a thin smile touching his lips. "You mean termination of my life? Because let’s be clear, Mr. Miller. If I leave, I’m not just a patient. "Mr. Miller," Chen said, her voice calm but firm. "Perhaps we should give Mr. Vale some time to recover. We can discuss the compensation terms later."

Miller looked at Chen. Chen looked at Miller. For a long moment, neither of them spoke. Then Chen nodded, a small, almost imperceptible movement.

Miller sighed. "Very well. But this is a one-time exception. And if you leak this information, we will terminate your participation and pursue legal action for breach of contract."

He turned to leave, then paused at the door. "The compensation terms will be finalized and sent to your account within the hour. Do not test us, Mr. Vale."

He left, closing the door behind him.
