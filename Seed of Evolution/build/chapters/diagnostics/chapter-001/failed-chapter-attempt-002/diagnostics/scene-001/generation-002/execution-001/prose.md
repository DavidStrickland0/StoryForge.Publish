# Chapter 1

The research hospital smelled like disinfectant and desperation.

Adrian sat in the sterile waiting area, his backpack heavy with textbooks he couldn't afford and his stomach growling with hunger he couldn't satisfy. The fluorescent lights hummed overhead, casting everything in a harsh, unflattering glow that made him look even thinner than he felt. He was nineteen years old, and he looked like he'd been starving for months.

Because he had been. Sort of.

His part-time job at the campus bookstore paid minimum wage. His student loans covered most of his tuition, but the remaining gap was a crushing weight. Rent ate up whatever he managed to save. Food came second. Always second.

He checked his phone again. The tuition payment was due in three days. His landlord wanted rent in a week. The email from the experimental-medicine program coordinator sat in his inbox, a lifeline thrown to a drowning man.

*We can offer competitive compensation for your participation in our clinical trials. Payment structure negotiable based on trial requirements.*

Competitive. Negotiable. Those words had echoed in his exhausted mind all night.

The receptionist called his name. "Mr. Vale? Dr. Thorne will see you now."

Adrian stood, his legs unsteady from too many hours on his feet and too little food in his stomach. He followed the receptionist down a hallway of white walls and closed doors until they reached a small office with a nameplate that read *Dr. Aris Thorne, Clinical Trial Coordinator.*

The door opened. Dr. Thorne was a woman in her late forties, with sharp features and sharper eyes. She wore a white lab coat over a gray blouse, her hair pulled back in a severe bun. She gestured to a chair across from her desk.

"Mr. Vale. Thank you for coming in on such short notice." Her voice was efficient, clinical. "I've reviewed your application. You're aware of the nature of the program?"

Adrian nodded. "The... the fertility study. Daily testing."

"Correct." Dr. Thorne slid a folder across the desk. "We'll need you to sign the consent forms. The terms are spelled out in detail, but I can summarize: daily visits for specimen collection, medication adherence, regular health screenings. In exchange, you'll receive payment that should cover your immediate financial obligations."

She paused, studying him with those sharp eyes. "The daily requirement is... intimate. Many applicants find it uncomfortable. Are you certain you wish to proceed?"

Adrian's throat felt tight. The humiliation of what he was agreeing to sat like a stone in his chest. But the alternative was dropping out. Losing everything.

"I'm certain," he heard himself say.

Dr. Thorne nodded. "Very well. I'll need you to review the consent forms carefully before signing. The legal language is dense, but it's important you understand what you're agreeing to."

She turned the folder toward him. The pages inside were covered in small print, clauses and conditions that made his eyes hurt to read. Payment structure. Breach penalties. Data usage rights. Emergency intervention authorization.

His hands trembled slightly as he reached for the pen she offered.
