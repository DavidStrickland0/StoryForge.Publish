## Revision Guidance

- Introduce escalating physical side effects and logistical challenges that force Adrian to make difficult choices about his food budget and time management.
- Develop the anomaly flag into a meaningful obstacle that changes Adrian's circumstances, available choices, or the route forward, rather than dismissing it without consequence.
- Remove redundant explanations of character motives and action meanings, and vary the sentence structure to avoid a choppy, disjointed rhythm.
- Account for Adrian's spending on food between days, which should reduce his available cash, to maintain consistency with his established financial desperation.
- Show progression in Adrian's physical condition by introducing new or more severe side effects, rather than repeating the same descriptions from Chapter 4.
- Clarify the payment structure: If the bonus is weekly, it should only be paid once per week, not on every day. If it is paid daily, the term 'weekly bonus' is misleading and should be changed to 'daily bonus' or 'compliance bonus'.
- Align the medication intake process with the established canon: If the participant is required to wait 15 minutes after taking the medication, this should be consistent across all chapters. If the participant leaves the clinic after taking the medication, the 15-minute wait should be removed or explained as a different requirement.
