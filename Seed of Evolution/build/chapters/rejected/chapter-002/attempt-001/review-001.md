## Overall Assessment

The chapter fails to advance the active Short arc by prematurely resolving the Medium arc's catastrophic accident and heroism milestones. It contains severe financial arithmetic errors, contradicting the established payment structure and cash flow. The narrative skips essential transitional events, creating a disjointed timeline and violating the required pacing for the current chapter target.

- Remove the roommate, as it contradicts the canon. Depict Adrian living alone in his tiny apartment.

- Use more varied and specific language to describe Adrian's internal state, avoiding repetitive, clichéd phrases. Focus on concrete actions and sensory details to convey his emotions.
