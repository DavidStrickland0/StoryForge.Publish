## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter re-performs the completed development of completing trial days (Chapters 4-5) without advancing the active Short arc's target for Chapter 6, which requires flagging significant anomalies and managing escalating side effects. It repeats the routine rather than progressing from the established state.
  Evidence: "The process was the same. Smooth. Controlled. Efficient." ... "Variance in cell count... It is within the acceptable range for the second day of treatment."
- [FinancialLogic] The financial arithmetic contradicts the previous chapter's established state. Chapter 5 ended with Adrian having $6 left after buying groceries. This chapter starts with him having $4, then spends $4 on bus/pastry, leaving $0, yet later claims he has $6 after his diner shift, ignoring the initial $4 balance or the $14 trial payment received earlier in the chapter.
  Evidence: "He had four dollars left in his wallet." ... "He had spent the four dollars on the bus fare and a small, stale pastry" ... "When his shift ended, he had six dollars in his pocket."
- [Continuity] The chapter describes the medication as requiring an empty stomach for absorption, which contradicts the established canon and previous chapters where Adrian takes the medication without such dietary restrictions or mentions of absorption protocols. This introduces a new, unsupported constraint on his routine.
  Evidence: "The medication required an empty stomach, or at least a light one, to ensure proper absorption. The instructions were clear."
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter fails to establish the 'significant anomalies' required by the Chapter 6 target. Instead, it repeats the 'slight variance' and 'within acceptable range' language from Chapter 5, failing to escalate the tension or change the clinic's response as mandated by the arc's progression.
  Evidence: "Significant variance in motility. Noted as positive." ... "It is within the acceptable range for the second day of treatment."
- [Character] Adrian's physical state is described as having 'tremors' and 'exhaustion' from walking, which contradicts the established canon of 'reduced fatigue' and 'increased physical strength' resulting from the nanite/medication interaction. The chapter reverts to his pre-transformation physical limitations.
  Evidence: "He was out of breath, not from exertion, but from the effort of suppressing the tremor in his hands." ... "If he walked... he would be exhausted"

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter lacks new narrative momentum, relying on repetitive descriptions of the trial process and Adrian's internal state without introducing new conflicts or developments.

## Revision Guidance

- Advance the narrative by introducing the significant anomalies in Adrian's samples as required by the Chapter 6 target.
- Correct the financial arithmetic to align with the previous chapter's ending balance and the current chapter's income/expenses.
- Remove the unsupported 'empty stomach' constraint on medication intake to maintain continuity with established canon.
- Update Adrian's physical description to reflect the established 'reduced fatigue' and 'increased strength' rather than reverting to exhaustion and tremors.
