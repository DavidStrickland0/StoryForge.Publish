# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter effectively establishes Adrian's desperation and the transactional nature of the trial enrollment. It successfully fulfills the Chapter 1 target by having Adrian sign the consent forms and receive his first payment. The narrative voice is consistent, and the power dynamic with Dr. Thorne is well-defined. No major continuity or SRD defects were found.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter concludes with a meta-narrative statement that breaks the fourth wall and spoils the immediate future, undermining the tension of the 'desperate measure' choice.
  Evidence: Tomorrow, he'd discover what he'd really signed up for.
- [Style] The narrative repeatedly explains Adrian's internal state and the meaning of his actions immediately after the dialogue or behavior has already conveyed it, resulting in redundant exposition.
  Evidence: The threat was clear. Adrian nodded. ... The pressure was subtle but effective. Adrian looked at the consent form...

## Engagement Findings

None.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the final sentence to maintain narrative immersion and avoid spoiling the immediate next step.
- Trim explanatory narration that restates the emotional impact of Thorne's dialogue or Adrian's physical reactions.
