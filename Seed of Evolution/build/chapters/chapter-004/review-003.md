# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter effectively executes the physical examination and blood draw, establishing the metabolic strain and financial tension. However, it contains a significant continuity error regarding the location of Adrian's funds, which contradicts the established state from the previous chapter. The narrative progression is otherwise sound, aligning with the active Short arc's target for Chapter 4.

## Findings

- [Continuity] The chapter states Adrian's money is in his backpack, but he was hospitalized after the accident and his belongings were not established as being with him in the recovery room.
  Evidence: The four hundred dollars in his backpack was already spoken for
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter completes the physical exam and blood draw but fails to establish the concrete next step for compensation, leaving the 'increased compensation' offer unresolved despite the target requiring clarification of terms.
  Evidence: Miller’s expression remained neutral. "We’re still negotiating the terms. I can assure you, the compensation will be fair."

## Engagement Findings

- The financial tension is well-maintained, but the resolution of the compensation ambiguity is deferred too long for the chapter's target.

## Revision Guidance

- Clarify the location of Adrian's funds or remove the reference to the backpack if he is in a hospital room.
- Ensure the chapter ends with a concrete next step regarding compensation or a specific obstacle to obtaining it, rather than just a promise of future negotiation.
