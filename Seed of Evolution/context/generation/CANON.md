# Canon

## Established Canon


- Adrian Vale's date of birth is March 14, 1999.


- Adrian Vale is enrolled in the male-fertility trial designated as Protocol 7-B.


- The trial requires Adrian Vale to take medication at 0800 hours daily.


- The trial requires Adrian Vale to report for specimen collection at 1000 hours daily.


- Deviation from the trial schedule results in a penalty deduction from compensation.


- Missing a day of the trial results in the loss of the week's pay.


- Non-compliance with the trial triggers a review process involving Dr. Thorne.


- Non-compliance with the trial carries the risk of being cut loose from the program.


- Adrian Vale's daily compensation for the male-fertility trial is fourteen dollars.


- The trial medication causes a persistent, low-level heat or burn sensation in Adrian Vale's chest.


- Adrian Vale's physical condition has changed to include increased sensory acuity, reduced fatigue, and heightened muscle tension.


- The trial facility records a 'slight variance in motility' in Adrian Vale's samples, which is noted as positive.


- The trial facility records a 'variance in cell count' in Adrian Vale's samples on the second day of treatment, which is noted as within the acceptable range.

## Changes Over Time


- Adrian Vale's physical state changed from chronic fatigue and joint ache to fluid movement, silent joints, and heightened sensory awareness.
