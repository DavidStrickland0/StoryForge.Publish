# ATWR — world starting facts

This is the same fantasy world, approached through a new independent adventure. Establish the new episode's setting and immediate problem on the page. Readers should not need Episode 1 to follow it.

The previous adventure involved a ruined city, an older temple, a separate shrine called the Gate of Silence, drainage channels, and a consuming temple barrier. The local feed was closed and independent observers at the watchfort corroborated the outcome. The companions handed over observation responsibility and reached an ordinary stopping point at the roadside Gilded Tankard. The ruined city is not declared universally safe, but no urgent obligation requires the cast to return there.

The Blight was dangerous corruption, associated with fog and creatures resistant to ordinary steel and capable of self-repair. The consuming barrier destroyed corruption brought into contact with it. Those are bounded established observations, not permission to make every new problem another Blight crisis or to solve it with an imported universal mechanism.

The forest keeper's full nature and its reference to a key remain uncertain background lore. That uncertainty is not an unfinished rescue, an active threat, or a compulsory sequel hook. Do not reopen it as the default premise.

Ordinary travel, supplies, money, fatigue, injuries, institutions, and personal choices matter. A new place, adversary, custom, or magic can be established when the approved episode premise needs it; do not invent an earlier off-page discovery to make the current scene work.

The discarded Silas Vane offer, Temple Springs cargo, alchemist deal, lunar-cycle explanation, sulfur-paste repellent, caravan departure, and future raider/guild/dragon campaigns are not established history or obligations for this episode.

## SRD foundations

Use SRD 3.5 race traits, class capabilities, skills, equipment constraints, and spell behavior as this world's ordinary adventuring baseline. Introduce new people and creatures with consistent traits; a role, appearance, or occupational title alone does not confer class features. Existing Blight phenomena and relic observations remain bounded accepted exceptions, not a replacement universal magic system. SRD-RULES.md records the episode's authority and limits.
