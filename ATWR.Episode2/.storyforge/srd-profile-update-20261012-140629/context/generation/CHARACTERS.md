# ATWR — recurring cast

## Starting authority

This is Episode 2, a new self-contained adventure starting at Chapter 1. The previous episode ended with all five companions safely recovering after the local Gate of Silence operation. These are starting character facts, not an imported chapter history or a new plot. The episode premise will specify the starting time and place. Allow credible recovery time if ordinary travel or strenuous work is required; do not erase wounds overnight.

## Elias Thorne

Practical, observant, and accustomed to coordinating the group. He carries a sword, dagger, leather armor, and the bronze plate map recovered in the earlier adventure. The map depicts the ruined city's temple, shrines, and channels; it is not a universal locator for the new episode. He has learned to accept help and share responsibility. His old side wound was treated and bandaged at the previous ending. Do not turn him into an invulnerable fighter, give him unestablished magic, or make every companion wait for his permission to think.

## Kaelen

A young mage whose established magic includes smoke and shields of light. His brass gauntlet is an older relic with an incompletely understood connection to the Blight. He can choose to leave it unworn; he is not secretly maintaining the previous episode's closed channel. He was exhausted by the stronger pull, then received care and rest. His limits and agency matter. Do not invent new powers, universal magical explanations, or an obligation to sacrifice him. His full history is not required reading for a new reader.

## Maren

A direct, pragmatic companion with a heavy hammer and established soldierly competence. She judges tools and plans by whether they work, and speaks more plainly than the others. Her hands were burned in the earlier operation and treated; her hammer's damaged wooden haft was replaced before the ending. Keep believable recovery constraints unless the new premise advances time. Competence is expressed in action rather than automatic obedience from everyone she addresses.

## Torin

A soldier, watchful and physically capable, carrying his established sword and ordinary travel equipment. He helps with practical logistics and shares the group's burdens. He can admit fear without losing competence. Give him decisions and observations of his own rather than making him a silent guard or a source of generic approval.

## Elara

A scholar who studies and tests rather than possessing a convenient answer to every new failure. She retains her chisel, notes, loose quartz dust, and satchel. The three working quartz fragments remain at the closed shrine in Episode 1; they are not in her pack. Her cut hand received ordinary care. Show new conclusions being earned from available evidence. She has not established a universal mosaic magic system, a lunar-cycle theory of the Blight, or the chemistry of a removed caravan plot.

## Relationships

The five are recurring companions with professional, non-romantic relationships as established. Preserve trust and individual agency without introducing permanent power escalation, romance, or a new series-wide dependency. Existing experience informs their decisions, but previous scenes should not be replayed to explain it. Give each speaker a concrete intention and a distinct response to the episode's problem.
