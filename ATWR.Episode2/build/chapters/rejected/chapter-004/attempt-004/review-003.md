## Revision Guidance

- Acknowledge Maren's pain and limited speed explicitly during movement.
- Stop Kaelen from casting if reserves are empty; show him resting.
- Advance the plot by resolving the immediate tactical situation or facing a new threat.
- Do not reset the scene to a planning state after rescue actions.
- Establish a clear budget for the party's fifty gold pieces spent on rations and supplies before entering the keep.
- Remove or significantly reduce the weight of Maren's broken cart axle to allow her to perform heavy labor without physical impossibility.
- Depict the cold environment as a genuine threat requiring active management (seeking warmth, conserving body heat) consistent with the described conditions.
- Clarify Kaelen's magical limitations to ensure he cannot produce multiple effects simultaneously when reserves are depleted.
