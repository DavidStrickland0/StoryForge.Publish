- Adjust Maren's physical capabilities to match her established injury and burden. Remove actions that require agility she cannot possess (jumping over tripwires) or show the consequences of pushing beyond her limits.
- Ensure the noise generated by the party's actions is consistent with their stealth objectives. If they make significant noise, either have goblins detect them or explain why the goblins are not alerted.
- Maintain consistency in structural descriptions. If stones are loose and removable in one scene, ensure similar stones behave consistently unless a specific reason for difficulty is provided.
