# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats narrative work completed in Chapter 3 (bypassing the pressure plate trap and slipping past the first goblin guard) without advancing the rescue objective. It introduces new traps and wards that contradict established continuity regarding the keep's defenses. The ending halts before resolving the rescue, failing to meet the Short arc's NextMilestone.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter relies on artificial suspense (waiting for patrol shift) rather than advancing the rescue objective or resolving the immediate threat, leading to a lack of reader engagement in the resolution phase.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the repeated trap bypassing and goblin guard encounter scenes; continue directly from Chapter 3's established state.
- Resolve the rescue objective by Chapter 9 as required by the Short arc; do not halt with a waiting period that contradicts the resolution criteria.
- Ensure Kaelen's capabilities align with Chapter 3 where he maintained light and confirmed ward status without immediate incapacitation.
- Remove the 'secondary ward' introduction to maintain continuity with the established broken ward state.
- Ensure Maren receives assistance (rope or help) before descending the stairs, or describe her managing the descent despite injury.
- Clarify whether the party actually passed through the hairline fracture or if they found an alternative route.
- Reconcile Kaelen's magical exhaustion: either establish a reason why opening a lock is significantly more taxing than maintaining a field, or adjust his stamina limits to be consistent with previous actions.
- Align the party's movement with their stated plan: if they decide to wait for a patrol shift, describe them waiting; if they move immediately, explain why they abandoned the waiting plan.
- Describe how the party maintains their position within the masked ward zone if such a constraint exists.
- Show Maren utilizing her ability to break the lock before deciding to wait, or explain why she chose not to use it despite having the capability.
- Track the fifty gold pieces from Chapter 1: describe how the party uses, saves, or spends this money during their journey.
