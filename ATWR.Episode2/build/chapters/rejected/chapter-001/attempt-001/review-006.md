# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains a significant financial logic defect regarding the payment amount and a narrative progression defect where the party repeats investigation actions already established by the premise.

## Findings

- [FinancialLogic] The chapter states the reward is fifty gold pieces, but later reveals only forty-five pieces are in the purse. The elder claims he counted it three times, yet five coins are missing without explanation. This contradicts the instruction to reconcile every description of a payment and not spend money twice.
  Evidence: "Reward of fifty gold pieces upon safe return..." ... "There are only forty-five pieces here. You've shorted us by five gold." ... "I counted it three times before bringing it to you. Fifty gold pieces is what we promised."
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the investigation and tracking actions described in the Short arc objectives for Chapters 1-3 (gathering intelligence, locating the keep) without advancing to the next milestone of entering the keep or developing a tactical plan. The party investigates the farm and tracks goblins again despite the premise stating these steps are part of the upcoming adventure flow.
  Evidence: "Goblins have been spotted raiding farms... Two adults remain missing..." ... "Let me see if I can identify any patterns in their behavior..." ... "We need to find it before they come back for more."
- [FinancialLogic] The party is presented with an advance payment of fifty gold pieces, but a subsequent count reveals only forty-five pieces, creating an unexplained deficit without establishing a mechanism for the loss.
  Evidence: The elder offered 'a small purse containing fifty gold pieces.' Later, Maren states, 'There are only forty-five pieces here. You've shorted us by five gold,' and Elara confirms, 'There are only forty-five pieces.'

## Engagement Findings

None.

## Revision Guidance

- Correct the purse count: either establish the elder stole five coins earlier or have the party discover a discrepancy that resolves naturally without contradicting the initial promise.
- Advance the narrative past the initial investigation phase. Move the party toward scouting the keep's perimeter or developing an entry plan as per the Short arc objectives.
- Establish a plausible mechanism for the missing five gold before the count occurs (e.g., a villager secretly taking coins, a trap stealing them, or the elder admitting to padding the purse earlier).
- Alternatively, reconcile the counts by having Elara verify the total matches the promised fifty gold and explain why Maren's initial count was wrong (e.g., miscounting due to distraction or poor lighting), ensuring the financial state remains consistent.
