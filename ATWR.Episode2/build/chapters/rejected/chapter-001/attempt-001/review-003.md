## Revision Guidance

- Split the narrative across nine chapters. Use Chapters 1-3 for investigation and tracking to the keep.
- Use Chapters 4-6 for infiltration, hazards, and reaching captives.
- Use Chapters 7-9 for rescue, combat, return, reward, and rest.
- Remove the immediate payment of fifty gold; establish a reason for delayed or partial payment.
- Adjust the timeline to reflect the recovery period without explicitly stating 'three weeks' if not supported by prior text.
- Introduce comic elements specific to the goblin raid (e.g., mundane failures, bureaucratic hurdles).
- Do not resolve the entire adventure in Chapter 1; leave hooks for subsequent chapters.
- Remove the scene where Elder Thorne hands over the fifty gold pieces as an 'advance payment' after the rescue is complete.
- Ensure the reward is only handed over at the very end of the chapter, after Nella and Hobb are safely returned to the village and the goblin threat is neutralized.
- If an advance payment is desired for narrative pacing, establish it explicitly as a partial sum (e.g., ten or fifteen gold) given before departure, with the remainder due upon successful completion.
