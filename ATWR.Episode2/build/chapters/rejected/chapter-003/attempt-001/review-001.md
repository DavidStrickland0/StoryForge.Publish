# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains a significant progression defect by re-performing the scouting and entry planning work completed in Chapter 2, rather than advancing to the rescue phase. It also introduces a new threat (Skrit's anger) at the end without establishing it through the chapter's primary action.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the scouting and tactical planning work completed in Chapter 2. The party re-discusses entry points, ward mechanics, and service passages that were already identified and analyzed in the previous finalized chapter.
  Evidence: "Neither," Elara said... 'This isn't a main thoroughfare. It's a service passage.' ... 'We go through the service passage,' Elias said.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter manufactures an artificial escalation by having Skrit declare his anger and threaten the party in the final scene, without earning this new threat through the primary action of rescuing the captives.
  Evidence: "He is angry." ... "Skrit smiled. 'Is that so? And what will you do about it?'" ... Elias charged.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter resolves the rescue objective but fails to establish the ResolutionCriteria for the Medium arc (returning safely with reward) before ending, leaving the story at an unstable stopping point.
  Evidence: "It's over,' Elias said. 'We're getting you out.' ... They moved up the stairs... Finally, they reached the top floor.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter ignores the active Short arc's NextMilestone (developing a tactical plan) by re-performing that work instead of executing the rescue and return.
  Evidence: "We go through the service passage,' Elias said. 'We avoid the main guard post... We enter the storage area from the side.'

  Evidence: "These wards are simpler than what we saw at the Gate of Silence,' Elara explained... 'But they still block detection spells.'

## Revision Guidance

- Advance the narrative by executing the rescue plan established in Chapter 2 rather than re-planning entry.
- Earn Skrit's anger through a failed stealth attempt or a discovered trap before the final confrontation.
- Conclude the chapter with the party returning to Pinehollow, receiving payment, and resting at the Crooked Stag.
- Add a brief mention of the party consuming rations or sharing a small meal before entering the keep to establish they have carried supplies.
- Alternatively, acknowledge that they are light on food due to the urgency of the mission and the broken cart axle slowing Maren, making the lack of provisions a consequence rather than an oversight.
