# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains a significant progression defect by re-performing the scouting and entry planning work completed in Chapter 2, rather than advancing to the rescue phase. Additionally, there is a continuity error regarding Kaelen's magical state.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the 'scouting and planning' phase established in Chapter 2 (identifying wards, analyzing maps, discussing entry points) instead of advancing to the rescue. The party spends the entire chapter debating how to enter a room they have already scouted, failing to materially change their circumstances or advance the Short arc's NextMilestone.
  Evidence: "Neither," Elara said... 'The passage behind the door,' she said, standing up slowly. She brushed a speck of dust from her sleeve. 'Torin, you mentioned earlier that the goblins use a specific route to move supplies...'
- [Continuity] Kaelen's magical state contradicts Chapter 2. In Chapter 2, he explicitly states, 'I've got enough left for smoke screens and maybe a shield.' This chapter later has him say, 'No. I have nothing left,' which erases the established resource availability without an intervening event that depleted his magic.
  Evidence: "Kaelen shook his head slowly. 'No. I have nothing left. If we need a distraction, I can make noise. But no magic.'"
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter manufactures artificial momentum by introducing a new threat (Skrit's anger) and a new location (the tower) solely in the final paragraphs without earning it through the chapter's primary action. The bulk of the chapter is spent on low-stakes planning that was already resolved.
  Evidence: "Finally, they reached the top floor... A single door stood at the end of the hallway... In the center sat a goblin, larger than the others... 'Ah,' he said, his voice deep. 'The rescue party. I was wondering when you'd show up.'"
- [FinancialLogic] The party proceeds to infiltrate a goblin keep and engage in combat without any established source of funds, despite the narrative implying they are operating as contracted adventurers who previously received payment for similar services.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter relies on artificial escalation (new threat at the end) rather than earning momentum through the primary action of rescuing captives.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the planning dialogue about the service passage; assume it is already known from Chapter 2.
- Have the party immediately enter the keep and move toward the captives to advance the arc.
- Adjust Kaelen's dialogue to reflect his remaining resources or establish a specific event that depleted them.
- Establish a clear financial state for the party at the start of the chapter. Either confirm they have received their fifty gold pieces from Chapter 1 and are carrying it, or explicitly state that they are operating on credit/deferred payment and explain how this impacts their ability to purchase necessities if needed later.
