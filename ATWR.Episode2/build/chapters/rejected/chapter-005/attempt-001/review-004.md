# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats completed developments from Chapter 4 (rescue, map reading, decoy plan) without advancing the Short arc's NextMilestone. It re-performs infiltration and confrontation actions already resolved. Financial logic is violated by implying Hobb is unconscious yet later 'awakes' without treatment or time passage. Physical coherence fails as Torin carries a fractured-leg captive up steep steps while injured, with no mechanical explanation for endurance.
