# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats narrative work completed in Chapter 4 (rescuing captives, assessing injuries, planning decoy sounds) and re-performs the same tactical decision-making regarding route selection without advancing the Short arc's NextMilestone. It also introduces a new threat (Skrit Ironjaw) at the end without establishing it through prior discovery or escalation within the chapter.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter suffers from repetitive pacing, recycling dialogue and tactical discussions already resolved in Chapter 4 without introducing new complications or discoveries that justify the repetition.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the repeated assessment of Hobb's condition and the re-planning of the decoy sound; these were completed in Chapter 4.
- Advance the plot immediately after the rescue by having the party execute their plan to exit or confront Skrit, rather than looping back to stealth discussions.
- Introduce Skrit Ironjaw through prior observation or a logical escalation of the goblin threat established in Chapter 4, not as an abrupt ending cliffhanger.
- Clarify the source of objects used for distraction (e.g., establish that loose gravel or coins were available earlier).
- Show Torin taking breaks or receiving aid when carrying Hobb becomes too painful.
- Describe Maren adjusting her grip or using a tool to relieve pressure from the broken axle on her shoulder.
- Explain how the group navigates narrow corridors with heavy burdens without triggering traps or alerting enemies.
- Resolve the contradiction between Kaelen's lack of magic and his ability to move normally.
- Clarify the goblin guard's behavior when ordered to open the door.
- Establish how the party bypasses the goblin guard at the upper level entrance.
- Introduce or establish a source of currency for the party if financial transactions are implied.
