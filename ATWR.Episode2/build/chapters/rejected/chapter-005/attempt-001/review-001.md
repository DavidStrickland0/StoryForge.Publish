# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats the exact corridor navigation and formation spacing from Chapter 4 without advancing the plot or resolving the immediate threat. It also introduces a new goblin guard and confrontation that contradicts the established state of the party having already secured the exit route and moved past the storage chamber.

The party is shown moving through the same 'narrow throat' and establishing the same formation spacing ('Elias kept his hand on Maren's shoulder... They were a single, breathing unit') that was already finalized.
  Evidence: "The corridor narrowed, the ceiling dropping until Torin had to tilt his head back... Elias kept his hand on Maren's shoulder... They were a single, breathing unit, moving through the dark with the slow, deliberate grace of water finding its level."
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter ignores the active Short arc's NextMilestone (securing the exit route and returning to the village) by stopping before the party leaves the keep.   Evidence: "It was Skrit Ironjaw... He looked bored... 'Ah,' Skrit said, his voice a low, rasping growl. 'The heroes.'"

  Evidence: "A single goblin stood before it... He held a spear in one hand and a crude club in the other. He was not panicking. He was waiting."

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter relies on artificial suspense (new goblin guard) rather than advancing the established goal of returning to Pinehollow for reward and rest.

## Revision Guidance

- Advance the plot by having the party navigate the exit corridor and reach the village tavern. Narrate the receipt of payment and meal at the Crooked Stag.
- Remove the Skrit confrontation scene or integrate it as a flashback if necessary, but prioritize completing the rescue arc's resolution criteria.
- Restore Kaelen's access to coins or explain why he lacks them despite established payment.
- Clarify whether Kaelen possesses the dried meat rind or remove the action of using it if he does not.
- Ensure descriptions of Kaelen's actions align with his established state of magical depletion and physical exhaustion.
