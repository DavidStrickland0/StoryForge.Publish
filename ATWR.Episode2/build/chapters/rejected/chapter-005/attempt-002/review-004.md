## Revision Guidance

- Advance the Short arc by confronting Skrit or securing the exit instead of moving a pot.
- Remove the unearned 'Water of life' vial production; use Elara's established stabilizer if needed.
- Resolve the arc by defeating/capturing the band or neutralizing raids before ending.
- Clarify whether the incline allows continuous rolling or if Elias must actively push it to maintain motion. If the pot rolls continuously without further input, describe how this is achieved physically (e.g., a ramp, a slope). If Elias must keep pushing, remove the 'sentient momentum' description that implies it moves on its own.
- Ensure the physics of the distraction align with the established environment: if the corridor has an incline, explain why the pot doesn't stop immediately when pushed; if it is flat, explain how Elias maintains its motion.
