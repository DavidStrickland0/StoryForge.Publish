# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats completed developments from Chapter 4 (layout discussion, goblin intimidation) without advancing the Short arc's NextMilestone. It introduces a new threat (Skrit's guards) at the end without earning it through prior escalation. Financial logic is violated by implying Hobb and Nella are carried out with no mention of supplies or cost. Style defects include repeated clipped sentences and excessive explanation of character states already evident from action.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the layout discussion and goblin intimidation sequence established in Chapter 4, failing to advance the Short arc's NextMilestone (confront Skrit). It re-performs completed narrative work instead of progressing toward the exit or resolution.
  Evidence: "Elara stepped forward... 'The layout is simple,' she said. 'We are in the cellar... If we move left, we pass through the open space. If we move right, we go through the door.'" "'Left is risky,' Maren countered. 'Open space means no cover...'"
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter introduces Skrit's guards and a new threat at the very end without establishing escalation or changing circumstances, violating the rule against manufacturing artificial momentum.
  Evidence: "Inside, two goblins stood guard... And behind them, sitting on a rough-hewn chair, was a goblin who was different... He held a sword... Skrit Ironjaw... surrendered."
- [FinancialLogic] The chapter implies the party carries injured captives out of the keep without accounting for supplies, food, or cost, violating financial plausibility regarding resources and obligations.
  Evidence: "Torin lifted Hobb again... Maren followed, guiding Nella... They moved toward the door... The air was cold and fresh... They were out."
- [Prose] The chapter uses repeated clipped narrative sentences (e.g., "He looked at his companions... He looked at his sword...") and excessive explanation of character states, violating style rules against habitual contrast reframing and unnecessary exposition.
  Evidence: "His shoulders slumped... His eyes were not the wild, yellow panic... He was a manager." "The tension in the room broke, not with a bang, but with a sigh."
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter resolves the arc by having Skrit surrender and the party leave without establishing ResolutionCriteria (reward collection, rest at Crooked Stag), contradicting the Medium arc's objective.
  Evidence: "Elias turned to his companions. 'Let's go,' he said... They reached the stairs... The stars were out... Elias took a deep breath... The victory. The end. The beginning."
- [PhysicalLogic] Elias claims to see Torin compensating for a shoulder injury by shifting weight, but he has no established visual evidence of this specific biomechanical detail in the dark corridor.
  Evidence: "Your back," Elias said. He pointed a gloved finger at the air beside Torin's head. "You're compensating. I see it in the way you shift your weight to the left. You're protecting your right side, or the shoulder, or something else that doesn't show in the light."
- [PhysicalLogic] Kaelen claims he can make a noise by dropping a stone or coin, but the text explicitly states his magical reserves are completely depleted and he cannot cast spells or generate light, making the 'magic trick' comparison physically inconsistent with his established state.
  Evidence: "I can't make light. But I can make noise... I can drop a stone. Or a coin... It will draw their attention to one specific point." ... "Kaelen dropped the flint. It struck the stone floor with a sharp, brittle clack..." (Contradicted by Canon: Kaelen's magical reserves are completely depleted; he cannot cast spells or generate light effectively.)

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the repeated layout discussion; advance directly to confronting Skrit or exiting.
- Earn the new threat by escalating tension earlier in the chapter.
- Add a scene showing resource management (food, supplies) when carrying captives.
- Replace clipped sentences with varied structure and remove explanatory narration.
- Extend the ending to include reward collection and rest at the Crooked Stag.
- Remove Elias's claim of seeing specific weight-shifting mechanics in the dark; replace with a general observation of Torin's uneven gait or audible strain.
- Clarify Kaelen's statement to emphasize he is using physical objects (flint/stone) rather than magic, and remove the 'magic trick' comparison which contradicts his depleted state.
