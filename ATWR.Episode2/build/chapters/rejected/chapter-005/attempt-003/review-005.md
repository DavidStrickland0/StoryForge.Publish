# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats narrative work completed in Chapter 4 (rescuing captives, assessing injuries, planning decoy sounds) without advancing the Short arc's NextMilestone of confronting Skrit or securing the exit. It also introduces a new threat (Skrit's guards and chief) at the end without establishing it through the chapter's primary action, violating pacing rules.

The primary action repeats the stealth exit plan and decoy sound tactic established previously rather than progressing to the confrontation with Skrit or the retreat.

## Revision Guidance

- Cut the re-navigating cellar scenes; start directly with the confrontation or retreat from Chapter 4's ending.
- Replace the manufactured Skrit encounter with a consequence of the previous chapter's actions (e.g., goblins regrouping, a trap triggering).
- Ensure the ending depicts a concrete resolution to the raid threat (capture, defeat, or forced disbandment) rather than a vague surrender.
- Advance the Short arc's NextMilestone immediately; do not spend primary action on repeating stealth tactics already established.
- Clarify how Kaelen acquires and holds the flint (e.g., picking it up from a pouch or pocket) to resolve the physical impossibility of dropping an object he cannot manipulate.
- Adjust Torin's movement description to reflect the strain of his injury, such as uneven steps, visible wincing, or reliance on Maren for support, rather than 'heavy, deliberate grace' and perfect balance.
