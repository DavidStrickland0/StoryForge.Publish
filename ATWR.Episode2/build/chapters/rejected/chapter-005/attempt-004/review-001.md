# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains a significant financial logic defect regarding the reward payment and a narrative progression defect where the party re-performs the stealth infiltration work already completed in Chapter 4.

## Findings

- [FinancialLogic] Elias takes 'silver coins' from the table, but the text states they are 'not enough to buy a new farm' yet implies they pay for the work. The narrative then describes them moving toward the exit and village without mentioning receiving the agreed fifty gold pieces or verifying the payment count, contradicting the contract terms established in Chapter 1.
  Evidence: "Elias took the key. He looked at the silver coins on the table. They were not enough to buy a new farm... But they were enough to pay for the work they had done. And that was enough. 'Let's go,' Elias said.'"
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the stealth infiltration mechanics (using sound decoys, moving through corridors with injured captives, managing goblin attention) which were fully resolved in Chapter 4. The party has already secured the exit route and neutralized defenses; re-navigating the keep's layout as if it is a new obstacle constitutes a progression defect.
  Evidence: "Kaelen tossed the flint... It skittered across the flagstones... The goblins froze... Elias moved... 'Now,' Elias whispered. Maren's hammer flashed... Torin was already at the door. He shoved it open..."
- [FinancialLogic] The party collects a 'small, leather-bound book' and 'silver coins' as their reward for rescuing two villagers and defeating a raiding band, yet the text explicitly states these funds are 'not enough to buy a new farm, or a new life,' implying an absurdly low value that contradicts the established contract payment of fifty gold pieces from Chapter 1.
  Evidence: On the table sat a pile of stolen goods: a wheel of cheese, a loaf of bread, a handful of silver coins... They were not enough to buy a new farm, or a new life, or a new world. But they were enough to pay for the work they had done.

## Engagement Findings

None.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the re-performance of the stealth infiltration sequence; have the party exit immediately after securing Skrit.
- Replace the vague 'silver coins' with a specific count of fifty gold pieces matching the contract, or explicitly state they are collecting the reward upon return to the village.
- Clarify the quantity and denomination of the silver coins collected (e.g., 'a handful of silver coins' is ambiguous; specify if it matches the fifty gold contract or a smaller partial reward).
- If the reward is intended to be small, explain why the goblin chief surrendered such a significant sum without retaining any value for himself or his band.
- Ensure the narrative distinguishes between 'silver coins' (low value) and 'gold pieces' (high value) if both are present, or adjust the description to match the contract terms established in Chapter 1.
