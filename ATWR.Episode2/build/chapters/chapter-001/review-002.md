# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter contains multiple material defects regarding financial logic, character continuity, physical coherence, and narrative progression. The party receives fifty gold pieces but immediately spends them on supplies before leaving the village, contradicting the established need to verify funds first. Maren's hammer is described as having a splintered handle despite being inspected and found sound earlier. The scene transitions abruptly from the tavern to a farmhouse without establishing travel time or distance. The chapter ends with the party already deep in the woods following tracks, failing to complete the Short arc target of locating the keep before Chapter 3.

## Findings

This contradicts the instruction to verify funds and separate received money from future obligations, creating a cash flow contradiction where they are simultaneously paid and broke.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter ends with the party already deep in the woods following tracks, failing to complete the Short arc target of locating the keep before Chapter 3.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the immediate spending of the fifty gold pieces; have them verify the count and store it securely for later use.
- Replace the description of Maren's splintered hammer with a reference to her new, sound haft.
- Insert a brief paragraph describing the journey from the tavern to the farmhouse to bridge the scene transition.
- End Chapter 2 with the party arriving at the ruined keep and scouting its perimeter, not already deep in the woods.
- Trim Elias's dialogue that explicitly confirms obvious contract terms or status changes.
- Clarify whether the fifty gold is a flat fee or if the 'five gold per head' is a separate future payment. If it is a future payment, have Elias note that only the base fee was advanced and the remainder will be paid upon successful rescue.
- If the elder intended to pay for all contingencies upfront, adjust the count in the text to reflect more than fifty gold (e.g., sixty or seventy) or have the elder admit he cannot pay the per-head bonus until the mission succeeds.
