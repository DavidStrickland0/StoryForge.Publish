## Engagement Findings

- The coin-counting sequence is overly mechanical and abstract, lacking sensory texture to make the moment vivid or engaging for the reader.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the detailed coin-counting scene; show Elias checking the pouch's weight or feeling its contents instead of counting rows.
- Replace the description of Maren's splintered hammer with a reference to her new, sound haft.
- Complete the final sentence to ensure the chapter ends on a coherent thought.
- Infuse dialogue with character-specific voices (e.g., Maren's blunt practicality) rather than generic adventurer speech.
- Clarify the source of the fifty gold pieces. Establish that the village has liquid cash reserves (e.g., from grain sales, previous taxes, or savings) rather than implying they can simply print money or that 'advance payment' automatically means immediate liquidity without context.
- Alternatively, adjust the contract terms to reflect a realistic scenario for a small farming community (e.g., a smaller advance, a promise of payment upon return, or a barter arrangement involving grain/livestock) if the village cannot afford fifty gold pieces upfront.
