# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats Chapter 2's completed scouting and entry planning as a new primary action, violating NarrativeDirection rules. It also introduces a pressure plate trap without prior establishment or mechanical justification, creating a FinancialLogic-style continuity error regarding the keep's layout.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter substantially re-performs Chapter 2's completed scouting and entry planning. The party has already identified the keep, wards, maps, and storage chamber in Chapter 2. This chapter repeats the decision-making process for entering the keep instead of advancing to rescue or confrontation.
  Evidence: "We have the maps... We know where the captives are... What we don't know is how to get there without tripping whatever they've set up to stop us." The party then spends the chapter planning entry again, which was already resolved in Chapter 2.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter ignores the active Short arc's NextMilestone by delaying action. The party has already located the keep and identified entry points in Chapter 2. This chapter stops before executing the rescue or confronting Skrit, merely repeating the planning phase without meaningful change.
  Evidence: "We're ready... Let's go find them." followed immediately by another scene of planning at a junction, showing no progression toward the rescue objective established in Chapter 2.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter manufactures an artificial obstacle (pressure plate) solely to create tension. The keep's layout was already mapped in Chapter 2; introducing a new, unestablished trap mechanism contradicts the established continuity and feels manufactured rather than earned.
  Evidence: "It's a pressure plate... If you step on that spot, the mechanism behind the door shifts." This trap appears without prior setup or logical connection to the keep's known defenses from Chapter 2.
- [Canon] The chapter contradicts established canon by introducing a pressure plate trap in a room already mapped and explored in Chapter 2. The storage chamber was identified as having no active traps beyond wards; this new mechanism violates continuity.
  Evidence: "There's a draft... It's coming from under the floor... It's a counterweight." This contradicts Chapter 2's established layout where the party had already assessed the room and found only ward lines, not hidden mechanical traps.
- [Canon] The chapter repeats Chapter 2's completed development of locating the goblin camp and identifying its layout. The party has already confirmed the keep as a base camp, identified wards, and mapped routes in Chapter 2. Repeating this work violates continuity.

  Evidence: "Pressure plates are easy to break... If we pull it out, the floor structure changes." This contradicts Chapter 2's description of the room containing only "loose stones and debris from earlier raids."

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter fails to advance the narrative by repeating planning instead of executing the rescue.
- The chapter introduces an artificial obstacle (pressure plate) solely to create tension without prior setup.

- Remove the unestablished pressure plate trap or establish it in Chapter 2 if it's essential to the plot.
- Ensure Kaelen's magic depletion is consistent with his earlier statements about available resources.
- Avoid repeating information already established in Chapter 2, such as the raid schedule or keep layout.
- Use Elara's quartz dust consistently for its established purpose of detecting ward signatures.
- Replace the physical explanation for disabling the pressure plate with a method that relies on established mechanics (e.g., lifting the stone to reveal a gap in the trigger line, using a lever to shift the weight off the plate, or simply stepping around the loose block if it is not part of the trigger assembly).
