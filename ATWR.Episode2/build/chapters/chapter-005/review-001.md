# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats narrative work completed in Chapter 4 (rescuing captives, neutralizing immediate threats) without advancing the Short arc's NextMilestone. It introduces a new threat (dozens of goblins) and a confrontation with Skrit solely at the end to manufacture artificial momentum. Financial logic is violated by implying Hobb is unconscious and stable after receiving 'water of life' which only stabilizes shock, not curing fractures or restoring consciousness.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter substantially re-performs the rescue objective established in Chapter 4. The party has already located and freed Nella and Hobb; this chapter repeats the extraction process as if it were new work rather than progressing from that state.
  Evidence: "Maren helped Nella to her feet... Maren knelt beside Nella... She produced a pair of heavy, curved blades—shears designed for cutting thick leather. She knelt beside Nella... The rope fell away." (Chapter 4) vs Chapter 5's repeated extraction sequence.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter manufactures escalation by introducing 'dozens' of goblins and a confrontation with Skrit solely in the final paragraphs without earning it through prior exploration or discovery. This violates the rule against introducing new threats only at the ending.
  Evidence: "And there were goblins. Dozens of them... In the center of the room, a large fire burned... And there were goblins. Dozens of them." (Chapter 5)
- [FinancialLogic] The chapter implies Hobb is unconscious and stable after receiving 'water of life,' which contradicts the established fact that this item only stabilizes shock and does not cure injuries or restore consciousness.
  Evidence: "Torin's face was grim... The cart driver was unconscious, his breathing shallow but steady, the water of life having done its quiet, vital work." (Chapter 5)
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter ignores the active Short arc to pursue unrelated activity. Instead of securing the exit route or returning to the village as per the NextMilestone, it focuses on a prolonged dialogue scene with Skrit that delays the primary objective.
  Evidence: "Elias raised his voice... 'Skrit,' he called out... The silence that followed was heavy..." (Chapter 5)
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter resolves an arc without establishing its ResolutionCriteria. It depicts the defeat of Skrit and the binding of goblins but fails to show the party returning to Pinehollow, collecting the reward, or resting at the Crooked Stag.
  Evidence: "We are done here," Elias said... They moved out of the hall... As they reached the door, Elias paused." (Chapter 5)
- [FinancialLogic] Torin carries Hobb (an unconscious adult cart driver) up a narrow, spiraling stone staircase while maintaining balance and endurance, which is physically implausible given the weight distribution and lack of mechanical aid.
  Evidence: Torin was already moving. He had shifted Hobb’s weight, adjusting the man’s limbs to distribute the load more evenly across his back... The cart driver was unconscious, his breathing shallow but steady... Torin’s face was grim, a bead of sweat tracing a line down his temple despite the chill in the air. He didn’t look at his shoulder. He didn’t need to. He simply began to climb.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter relies on artificial suspense by introducing a large goblin horde and Skrit solely at the end without prior setup or discovery.

## Revision Guidance

- Advance the Short arc's NextMilestone immediately after Chapter 4 by showing the party securing the exit route.
- Remove the repeated extraction sequence; assume Nella and Hobb are secured based on Chapter 4 evidence.
- Replace the 'dozens of goblins' reveal with a description of the keep's upper levels or the journey back to the village.
- Clarify that Hobb remains unconscious due to his fracture, not because the water of life cured him.
- End the chapter at a natural stopping point, such as reaching the village gate or arriving at the tavern.
- Modify the scene to show Torin using a rope or harness to secure Hobb's weight before climbing, or have another character assist in carrying Hobb up the stairs.
- Alternatively, describe Torin struggling significantly with each step, emphasizing the physical toll of carrying an unconscious adult up a narrow staircase.
