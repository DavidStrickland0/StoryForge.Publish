# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter fails FinancialLogic by having Torin carry an unconscious Hobb up a steep spiral staircase without accounting for the significant energy expenditure or the physical impossibility of maintaining that load over distance. It also violates NarrativeDirection by resolving the Short arc's confrontation and exit objectives in Chapter 5, which contradicts the active status of 'medium-001-009-short-2' requiring further infiltration work before resolution.

## Findings

- [FinancialLogic] The chapter implies Torin carries an unconscious Hobb up a steep, spiraling staircase. This requires immense caloric expenditure and physical stamina that contradicts the established state of the party's depleted resources and injuries. Carrying a heavy, limp burden up multiple flights without stopping for rest or food is physically implausible given the prior exhaustion described.
  Evidence: Torin was already moving... He simply began to climb. The staircase was narrow, a spiraling throat of stone that wound upward into the darkness... Halfway up, Torin stopped.
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter resolves the primary Short arc objective (rescue and confrontation) in Chapter 5. The active Short arc 'medium-001-009-short-2' is marked as Active with a NextMilestone to secure the exit route, yet this chapter completes the rescue, neutralizes the chief, and initiates departure. This repeats completed narrative work rather than advancing toward the defined milestone.
  Evidence: "We are done here," he said. "Let's go." They stepped out of the hall... The goblins watched them go.
- [FinancialLogic] Elara produces hemp rope from her satchel to bind goblins, but this action contradicts established continuity where Elara's satchel contains charcoal, quartz dust, and a vial of water; she does not possess a supply of thick hemp rope.
  Evidence: "Elara nodded, her hand moving to her satchel. She produced a length of thick, coarse hemp... It was rough, abrasive, and effective. She held it out to Maren, who took it with one hand, her other arm still supporting Nella."

## Engagement Findings

None.

## Revision Guidance

- Reduce the physical toll of carrying Hobb up the stairs by having Torin rest or use a pulley system if available.
- Extend the chapter to include navigating traps or upper-level hazards before the final confrontation with Skrit.
- Have Elara retrieve the rope from a different source (e.g., Maren's pack, Torin's gear, or a pre-stashed supply in the keep) to resolve the continuity error regarding her satchel contents.
