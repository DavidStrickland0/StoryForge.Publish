# Chapter Review

## Overall Assessment

The chapter repeats Chapter 1's contract acceptance and initial investigation as if they are new events, violating NarrativeDirection rules. It also introduces a mechanical violation by having Kaelen cast detect magic without established spell access or resources, contradicting SRD continuity.

## Findings

- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter repeats the acceptance of the contract and the initial investigation at Nella's farmhouse as if these events have not occurred. Chapter 1 already established the contract was signed, payment verified, and the party departed to investigate. This chapter re-performs the same actions without advancing the narrative from that established state.
  Evidence: "The elder reached into his pocket and pulled out a small leather pouch... Fifty gold pieces exactly." ... "Elias picked up the pouch and set it aside carefully, checking its weight before opening it to count the coins inside. One by one, he laid them out on the table, arranging them in neat rows of five. There were ten rows of five, making fifty gold pieces total."
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter treats the discovery of goblin tracks and the decision to follow them as new developments, ignoring that Chapter 1 already concluded with the party identifying tracks and leaving the farmhouse to pursue the trail. The narrative fails to progress from the state established in the previous finalized chapter.
  Evidence: "These are wider, deeper, and show partial claw marks along the edges... Goblins," she confirmed flatly." ... "Let's walk to the nearest farmhouse, check the scene, and see what we can carry back with us."
- [SRD] Kaelen casts detect magic without established access or resources. The character sheet defines him as a mage with bounded observed abilities (smoke, shields), not granting full spell lists. Casting detect magic implies a prepared wizard or sorcerer with available slots, which contradicts the established limitation that he is not automatically a full caster.
  Evidence: "Kaelen hesitated briefly before raising his hand and casting detect magic in its most basic form—a faint shimmer of golden light radiating from the gauntlet as it scanned the area."
- [FinancialLogic] The chapter implies the party has sufficient resources to purchase supplies immediately after receiving fifty gold, yet later describes Maren carrying a broken cart axle and Elias worrying about limited time/energy without addressing how they acquired food or other necessities. The financial state is inconsistent with the established need for careful resource management.
  Evidence: "We'll use these for supplies before we leave." ... "The group had learned quickly over the years that relying too heavily on magical solutions could leave them vulnerable if resources ran low."
- [Canon] The chapter references 'previous operation' burns and a 'broken cart axle' found earlier, but Chapter 1 explicitly states Maren's hammer haft was replaced before the ending and does not mention a broken axle being carried. This contradicts established continuity regarding equipment status.
  Evidence: "Maren adjusted the broken cart axle strapped across her shoulder..." ... "her hammer resting casually in one hand." (Chapter 1 establishes the haft was replaced)."
- [Character] Elara uses quartz dust to reveal ward patterns, but Chapter 1 states the three working quartz fragments remain at the closed shrine in Episode 1 and are not in her pack. She cannot access them for this investigation.
  Evidence: "She reached into her satchel and pulled out a small vial of loose quartz dust..." (Contradicts Canon.md: "The three working quartz fragments remain at the closed shrine in Episode 1; they are not in her pack.")"
- [NarrativeDirection] The chapter ends with the party still planning entry into the keep, having failed to complete the rescue or return for reward. This contradicts the Short arc target for Chapter 3 which requires establishing a viable plan of entry, but the chapter spends its primary action repeating earlier investigation steps rather than executing the planned entry.
  Evidence: "We proceed into the deeper chambers now? Or do we wait until morning..." ... "Then we'd better hurry up." (No actual entry or rescue occurs)."
- [FinancialLogic] The party is described as having received fifty gold pieces for supplies, yet they are simultaneously depicted as actively looting and carrying away significant quantities of stolen goods (grain sacks, tools, etc.) from the goblin keep without any mention of these items being added to their inventory or affecting their financial status.
  Evidence: Elias said: 'We'll use these for supplies before we leave.' ... Inside this new room... Stacks of looted goods—grain sacks, metal tools, and farming implements—lined one wall... Another section housed shelves stacked high with stolen goods: baskets of eggs, jars of pickled vegetables, bundles of woolen clothing taken directly from farms outside the keep.
- [PhysicalLogic] Maren is described as carrying a 'broken cart axle' strapped to her shoulder while also managing a heavy hammer and dealing with rain-soaked conditions, which creates an implausible physical burden for a single character without explicit mention of assistance or mechanical advantage.
  Evidence: She adjusted the broken cart axle strapped across her shoulder... Her hands, wrapped in leather and still sensitive from the burns of the previous operation, gripped her hammer's haft with a tightness that betrayed her discomfort.

## Engagement Findings

- The chapter relies on artificial suspense by repeating investigation steps instead of advancing the plot toward the keep's interior.
- The ending feels unresolved because the party has not yet entered the keep or rescued captives, despite Chapter 3 targets expecting a plan of entry.

## Revision Guidance

- Remove the contract signing and initial farmhouse investigation scenes; start directly with tracking in the woods as per Chapter 1's ending.
- Replace Kaelen's detect magic with established abilities like smoke screens or sensory checks consistent with his bounded mage role.
- Ensure Elara does not use quartz dust from her pack; describe her using other methods to analyze wards.
- Clarify Maren's equipment status: either remove the broken axle reference or explain how she acquired it if it was a new injury.
- Advance the narrative by having the party actually enter the keep and confront goblins rather than just planning entry.
- Clarify whether the fifty gold pieces received are intended to cover all supplies or if the party is expected to purchase additional items. If they are looting goods, describe how these items are being transported (e.g., left behind, carried by multiple characters) to avoid implying impossible physical strain.
- Reduce Maren's physical load by having another character assist her with the broken cart axle or by having her leave it behind if it is not essential to the mission.
