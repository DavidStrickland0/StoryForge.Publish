[F118] He stood up, brushing the dust from his knees. "Torin, keep watching. Kaelen, save your energy. Maren, stay close. Elara, keep listening."

[F119] He looked at the iron-bound door at the far end of the hub, a dark rectangle against the dim light. Beyond it, Nella and Hobb were waiting. And beyond them, Skrit Ironjaw was waiting too.

[F120] "We’re ready," Elias said, though the word felt heavy, laden with the weight of what was to come. "Let’s go find them."

[F121] The iron-bound door stood between them and the rest of the keep, its surface pitted with rust and pockmarked by old knife strikes. It was not locked, but it was not open, either. [F122] Elara knelt beside the threshold, her lantern casting a weak, trembling pool of light across the flagstones. She did not look at the door. She looked at the floor.

[F126] Maren crouched beside her, her hammer resting on her knee. Her face was tight with concentration, the pain in her shoulder a dull, persistent throb that she had learned to ignore. She looked at the floor, then at the door, then at the rough-hewn stone wall to the left of the entrance.
