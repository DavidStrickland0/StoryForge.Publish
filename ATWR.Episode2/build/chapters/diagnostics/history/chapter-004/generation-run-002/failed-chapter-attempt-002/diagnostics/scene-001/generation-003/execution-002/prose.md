The corridor stretched ahead, a throat of damp, cold stone that seemed to widen slightly as they moved deeper. Elias Thorne paused at the entrance to the outer wall breach, his hand resting on the hilt of his sword rather than gripping it in alarm. The hole was messy—chunks of masonry torn loose by some unseen force, perhaps goblin dynamite or sabotage years ago—but it had held them through the rainstorm and the initial infiltration. It would not hold them forever if they lingered near the edge.

"Torin," Elias said, keeping his voice low so it did not carry off the breach's crumbling rim. "We need to get inside the keep proper before we draw attention. The guard post is just beyond that archway."
